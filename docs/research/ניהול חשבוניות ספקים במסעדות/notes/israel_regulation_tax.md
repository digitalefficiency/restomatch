## 1. "חשבוניות ישראל" (Israel Invoices / CTC reform) and allocation numbers (מספר הקצאה)

### Takeaway
Since 1 June 2026, a restaurant (the buyer) may **not deduct input VAT (ניכוי מס תשומות)** on any supplier tax invoice above **₪5,000 before VAT** unless the invoice has a valid Tax Authority **allocation number (מספר הקצאה)**. The threshold fell from ₪25,000 (May 2024) → ₪20,000 (Jan 2025) → ₪10,000 (Jan 2026) → ₪5,000 (Jun 2026). The ₪5,000 step was pulled forward from the original January 2028 date. For a restaurant, most mid-size weekly supplier invoices (meat, fish, beverages, consolidated monthly invoices) now fall inside the regime.

### Cited Findings
**Threshold schedule (invoice amount before VAT, the rule applies to invoices *above* the threshold):**
- May 2024 launch: ₪25,000; 1 Jan 2025: ₪20,000; 1 Jan 2026: ₪10,000; 1 Jun 2026: ₪5,000. — [iCount guide](https://www.icount.co.il/blog/invoice-israel/); [BaseCRM 2026 guide](https://basecrm.co.il/guides/allocation-number-invoices-2026/); [Federation of Chambers of Commerce (לשכת המסחר)](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/finance/1429/162557/); [Yanir Farkash & Co. CPA](https://www.yfcpa.co.il/newsletter/%D7%94%D7%97%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%94-1-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99-2026-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%94-%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%9E/)
- KPMG (Dec 2025): the Israel Tax Authority (ITA, רשות המסים) published a new regulation on **7 December 2025** setting ₪10,000 from 1 Jan 2026 and ₪5,000 from 1 Jun 2026 for B2B invoices, excluding VAT. — [KPMG TaxNewsFlash, Dec 2025](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/12/tnf-israel-expansion-of-mandatory-e-invoicing-model.html)
- The **original** schedule (pre-acceleration) was ₪10,000 in January 2027 and ₪5,000 in January 2028; the ITA confirmed bringing completion forward from January 2028 to June 2026. — [Sovos](https://sovos.com/vat/tax-rules/e-invoicing-israel/); [vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/israel/israel-e-invoicing-budget-proposals/); [VATupdate, Apr 2025](https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/04/20/israel-advances-timeline-for-allocation-number-requirements/)
- One source attributes the gradual threshold reduction to the Economic Efficiency Law for 2025 (חוק ההתייעלות הכלכלית לשנת 2025). — [vuz.co.il guide](https://vuz.co.il/knowledge-center/israel-invoices-allocation-number-guide-2026/) (vendor source, not verified against statute)
- Threshold is measured **before VAT**; worked example: an invoice of ₪5,600 including VAT is ≈₪4,745 before VAT and is below the line. — [Slate guide (English)](https://www.slate.co.il/en/guides/mispar-hakzaa)

**Legal basis and consequence for the buyer:**
- Without an allocation number, the invoice recipient may not deduct the VAT included in it, under **section 38(א1) of the VAT Law (חוק מס ערך מוסף)**. — [Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/finance/1429/162557/); [Grant Thornton Israel reminder (2026)](https://www.grantthornton.co.il/insights1/tax-insignths/2026/Tax_Invoice/); [Raveh-Ravid & Co.](https://www.raveh-ravid.co.il/bills/)
- One CPA summary describes the rule as a **temporary provision (הוראת שעה)** in section 38 running **1.1.2024 – 31.12.2028**. — [Raveh-Ravid & Co.](https://www.raveh-ravid.co.il/bills/) / [Grant Thornton Israel](https://www.grantthornton.co.il/insights1/tax-insignths/2026/Tax_Invoice/) (the search summary did not say which of the two listed it; verify against statute)
- A parallel provision, **section 38(א2)**, denies input VAT on an invoice for a transaction that breached the Cash Law (חוק לצמצום השימוש במזומן) and drew a monetary sanction. — [Income Tax Circular 03/2026 (gov.il PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/professional-directives-090226-1/he/IncomeTax_professional-directives-090226-1.pdf); [y-tax summary of Circular 03/2026](https://y-tax.co.il/income-tax-circular-03-2026-black-economy/)
- The ITA may **refuse** to allocate a number if it suspects the invoice was issued unlawfully (fictitious invoice, חשבונית פיקטיבית); one source dates this power to 2025. — [Grant Thornton Israel](https://www.grantthornton.co.il/insights1/tax-insignths/2026/Tax_Invoice/); [vuz.co.il](https://vuz.co.il/knowledge-center/israel-invoices-allocation-number-guide-2026/)
- The buyer bears the loss: the allocation number is a condition of the *recipient's* deduction. — [LookupTax](https://lookuptax.com/tax-changes/israel/ctc-allocation-number-clearance-2026); [VATupdate, Jun 2026](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/06/20/israel-tightens-real-time-invoice-validation-with-lower-threshold-requirement/)

**Scope:**
- Covers domestic **B2B** tax invoices (חשבונית מס) and tax invoice-receipts (חשבונית מס/קבלה) where the buyer is a licensed dealer (עוסק מורשה) entitled to deduct input VAT; B2C, exports and exempt sales are out of scope. — [Slate guide](https://www.slate.co.il/en/guides/mispar-hakzaa); [einvoicestudio compliance map](https://www.einvoicestudio.com/compliance-map/il)
- **Credit invoices (חשבונית זיכוי):** most vendor guides say a credit invoice does **not** need an allocation number at any amount; one guide says it does above the threshold (conflict). — [cpas.org.il](https://cpas.org.il/%D7%91%D7%A7%D7%A9%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99/); [BookedUp](https://bookedup.co.il/guides/mispar-haktzaa-cheshboniot-israel-2026); contradicted by [ezbigboss guide](https://ezbigboss.com/%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%94-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C/)

**Who requests it and how:**
- The **issuer (supplier)** requests the number through its accounting/invoicing software (API) or through the app in the ITA **personal area (אזור אישי)**, including in batches. Manual invoice-book users can type invoice details into a dedicated app. The number is issued only for an invoice that already exists (number, date, full customer details, amounts). Personal-area use requires smart-ID authentication. — [cpas.org.il](https://cpas.org.il/%D7%91%D7%A7%D7%A9%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99/); [Green Invoice (חשבונית ירוקה) guide](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/israel-invoice/); [ITA clarification circular 84.2023 (via Brit CPA PDF)](https://britcpa.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/84.23.pdf)
- Sometimes the ITA does not return the number immediately and the document sits in a "pending allocation number" (ממתין למספר הקצאה) state; during system outages one guide advises waiting within the 14-day statutory invoice-issue window. — [BookedUp](https://bookedup.co.il/guides/mispar-haktzaa-cheshboniot-israel-2026); [Green Invoice](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/israel-invoice/)
- Search summaries mention that the **recipient** may "demand" an allocation number, but no source described a buyer-side request procedure. — [Grant Thornton Israel](https://www.grantthornton.co.il/insights1/tax-insignths/2026/Tax_Invoice/)

**Verification / API:**
- Official ITA document "מודל חשבוניות ישראל – תיאור ה-API's", **edition 2.0, July 2024**, describes two services: (1) obtain an allocation number for an invoice; (2) **retrieve the details of an invoice that has already been allocated**. — [gov.il API description PDF](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/hor-software-other/he/vat_software-houses-180724.pdf)
- Developers register on the ITA developer portal (SHAAM/שע"ם Open API), get ITA approval, and end users authenticate through **OAuth**. — [ITA Open API developer presentation, Nov 2023 (via btop.co.il)](https://btop.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%92%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%99-%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%9408-11-23.pdf); [Chamber of Commerce presentation 30.11.2023](https://www.chamber.org.il/media/168404/%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%92%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-30_11_23.pdf)
- Invoice data is sent to the ITA platform as **JSON**, and no digital signature is required. — [Flowie Israel e-invoicing page](https://www.get-flowie.com/e-invoicing/israel)

**Enforcement and context data:**
- The ITA frames the model as an anti-fictitious-invoice measure. One vendor source puts uncollected VAT from fictitious invoices at **~₪5 billion a year**. This figure is unverified. — [iCount](https://www.icount.co.il/blog/invoice-israel/) / [Green Invoice](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/israel-invoice/) (search summary did not pin which)
- Vendor commentary expects the next step to be a "zero threshold" (every tax invoice). **No date has been set.** This is speculative. — [BaseCRM](https://basecrm.co.il/guides/allocation-number-invoices-2026/)

### Inferences
- For a restaurant, the risk is concrete and in ₪: an invoice of ₪6,000 + 18% VAT (₪1,080 VAT) with no valid allocation number means ₪1,080 of input VAT cannot be deducted, a direct cash leak. A monthly consolidated invoice from a meat or beverage supplier will almost always exceed ₪5,000.
- The "retrieve details of an allocated invoice" API service suggests software could cross-check that a supplier's allocation number really belongs to that invoice (amount, VAT ID, date), which an OCR + reconciliation product could automate. Whether a *buyer's* software can call it on the buyer's behalf, and under what OAuth scope, needs confirmation in the full API spec.
- Because credit invoices may be exempt from allocation, a supplier who "fixes" an over-billed invoice with a credit note does not touch the original allocation. The buyer's books must net the two documents correctly.

### Gaps
- No primary statute text was read. The exact wording of s.38(א1), the sunset date, and the exact legal vehicle for the 7 Dec 2025 regulation are unconfirmed.
- No source gave a **buyer-side remedy** (for example, can the restaurant itself request an allocation number if the supplier did not, and within what deadline). Not found.
- No official ITA enforcement statistics (numbers issued, refusals, assessments for missing numbers) were found.
- The public verification method (a portal lookup by allocation number?) for a buyer without API integration was not confirmed.

## 2. VAT basics that hit restaurants (rate, fruit & vegetables, deduction timing, consolidated invoices, credit invoices, document types)

### Takeaway
The standard VAT rate is **18% since 1 Jan 2025**. Fresh, unprocessed fruit and vegetables on the ministerial lists are **zero-rated (מע"מ בשיעור אפס, s.30(א) VAT Law)**, not exempt. A Finance Ministry plan for 2027 proposes cancelling this, but it is not approved. Input VAT must be deducted within **6 months** of the invoice date. Suppliers may issue one **consolidated monthly tax invoice (חשבונית מס מרכזת / ריכוז)** referencing delivery notes (תעודות משלוח), no later than month-end. Errors and cancellations are fixed by a **credit invoice (חשבונית זיכוי, s.49 VAT Law)**.

### Cited Findings
- VAT rate **18% from 1 January 2025** on domestic transactions and imports (was 17%). — [Efraty CPA, 2025 year-start prep](https://www.efraty.com/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99/%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94/%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94-2024/%D7%94%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A4%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%97%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA-2025-%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA-2024/); [Keep.co.il, "VAT in the 18% era"](https://keep.co.il/blog/nihul-maam-2026.html); [Kintsugi Israel VAT guide 2026](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/middle-east/israel)

  - Conflict: one search summary called it an "exemption (פטור)", and Avalara lists fruit & veg under both 0% and exempt and still shows 17%, so it is outdated. Treat it as **zero rate**. — [Avalara VATlive](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/vatlive/country-guides/africa-and-middle-east/israel/israeli-vat-rates-and-compliance.html)
  - Designer gift packs of fruit/veg raise a VAT-treatment question (a packaging/processing boundary). — [Capitax](https://www.capitax.co.il/content/2/1895)
- **Forward-looking:** the Finance Ministry is reported to be preparing, for **2027**, cancellation of the zero rate on fruit & vegetables (and the Eilat VAT exemption). This is a planned proposal, not approved law. — [Mako (~May 2026)](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-money/Article-542e6390e312e91026.htm); [VATupdate, 14 May 2026 (citing Ynet)](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/05/14/israel-considers-ending-vat-exemptions-on-eilat-produce-tourism-and-tax-benefits/). - **Invoice issue timing:** under **s.46 VAT Law**, a tax invoice is issued within **14 days of the tax event (מועד החיוב)**, not at the business's convenience or the next VAT filing. — [Eddie CPA, invoice timing](https://www.eddiecpa.com/articles/%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%93-%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%9C%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%9B%D7%9D-%D7%91/); [Hilan, VAT Law ch. 9 (invoices)](https://www.hilan.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%96-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2/%D7%97%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9A-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A3/%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A7-%D7%98-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA/)
- **Consolidated invoice (חשבונית מס מרכזת):** a dealer may issue one consolidated tax invoice **per month** to a given customer covering all that month's transactions, **listing the delivery-note numbers (or transaction-invoice numbers)**. It is issued during that month and **no later than the last day of the month**, and is included in that month's VAT reporting. This comes from an older (~8-year-old) tax encyclopedia; the regulation text was not seen. — [Hashavim tax encyclopedia (kolmas.net)](https://www.kolmas.net/encyclopedia/Entry/558)
- Market practice: at the end of the agreed period the delivery notes are summed into one tax invoice, usually dated at month-end. — [Rivhit (delivery documents)](https://invoice.rivhit.co.il/delivery_document); [Hashavshevet cloud help](https://home.wizcloud.co.il/help/summinvoice/)
- **Delivery note (תעודת משלוח):** under the bookkeeping instructions a delivery note must be prepared for every shipment of goods from the business unless an invoice is prepared at the same time. A delivery note need not show prices; a tax invoice must. — [Income Tax (Bookkeeping) Instructions 1973 (Nevo)](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/255_179.htm); [ezbigboss](https://ezbigboss.com/%D7%94%D7%91%D7%93%D7%9C-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A1/)
- **Credit invoice (חשבונית זיכוי):** rules for correcting an invoice are in **s.49 VAT Law** and cover a transaction that was not carried out or was cancelled. Returned goods after a tax invoice require a credit invoice for the returned items. — [Eddie CPA, receiving a tax invoice](https://www.eddiecpa.com/articles/%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%AA%D7%9D-%D7%97%D7%99%D7%99%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9C%D7%93%D7%A2%D7%AA-%D7%9B%D7%93%D7%99/)
- Document-type overview (tax invoice, transaction invoice (חשבונית עסקה), receipt (קבלה)) as an official procedure page. — [Kol Zchut, issuing tax invoice / transaction invoice / receipt](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%AA_%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A1,_%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA_%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%94_%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%94) (content not retrieved)

### Inferences
- A produce supplier's invoice should show raw fruit/veg lines at **0% VAT** and any processed lines (peeled, cut, pre-made salads) at 18%. A mis-rated line is both a VAT-compliance issue and a price-check signal. If the 2027 cancellation passes, produce costs for restaurants would rise by up to 18% gross, with input VAT deductible for an עוסק מורשה. That is a cash-flow and menu-pricing issue more than a net-cost issue.
- The consolidated-invoice model (many delivery notes → one month-end invoice) is the legal backbone of the "delivery note vs invoice" reconciliation that restaurants struggle with. Each consolidated invoice must reference its delivery notes, so matching is legally anchored.
- The 6-month deduction window creates a hard deadline: an invoice stuck in a dispute (wrong price, missing allocation number) for more than 6 months risks permanent loss of its input VAT.

### Gaps
- Whether the Director can extend the 6-month deduction window (and to how long) was not confirmed.
- The exact regulation number for the consolidated invoice, and whether month-end is still the limit, need checking against the current VAT Regulations (תקנות מע"מ).
- Rules for cancelling a **duplicate** tax invoice (vs. a cancelled transaction) were not specifically found.
- The definitions and legal uses of "חשבונית מס/קבלה" vs "קבלה" were not retrieved from an official page.

## 3. Bookkeeping instructions (הוראות ניהול ספרים) and digital archiving/scanning

### Takeaway
Since **1 Jan 2013**, the Income Tax (Bookkeeping) Instructions 1973 allow third-party source documents (supplier invoices, delivery notes, credit notes, receipts, orders) to be **scanned and the paper destroyed**. Conditions: the document is recorded in the accounting system; it is scanned to a legible identical image; it is marked "מסמך סרוק" (scanned document); it is electronically signed; and it is kept in a **digital archive (ארכיב דיגיטלי)** meeting Appendix ז rules (backup, technological continuity). Computerized accounting software must be **registered with the ITA (תוכנה רשומה)**.

### Cited Findings
- Legal basis: **הוראות מס הכנסה (ניהול פנקסי חשבונות), תשל"ג-1973**. Scanning and document-destruction rules are in **Appendix ו (נספח ו')**; digital-archive rules are in **Appendix ז (נספח ז')**. The scanned file is kept as an inseparable part of the accounting system. — [Nevo, Bookkeeping Instructions](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/255_179.htm); [Boaz Mekler CPA, scanning & digital archive](https://www.mekler.co.il/links/item/3139-%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%9C-%D7%A4%D7%A0%D7%A7%D7%A1%D7%99-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%E2%80%93-%D7%A1%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%95%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99.html); [cpa.co.il, digital-archive bookkeeping](https://www.cpa.co.il/%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%9C-%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99/)
- In force from **1 January 2013**; the ITA circular is cited as **9/2013** (one source gives the reference 933502, published 05.06.2013). — [Income Tax Circular 9/2013 PDF (ed1.co.il)](https://www.ed1.co.il/wp-uploads/ed1.telips.com/16481.pdf); [Goldpro CPA](https://www.goldpro.co.il/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%9B%D7%9F/%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94/%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%9C-%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A1%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%9E/)
- Conditions for a valid scan: recorded in the accounting system; scanned on a computerized scanner to give a legible output identical to the original; the words **"מסמך סרוק"** added; the scanned file **electronically signed**. Sources conflict on whether one signature or **two** (one of which may be automatic) attesting identity with the original are required. — [Payper, scanning regulations](https://payper.co.il/marketing/article/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94_%D7%9C%D7%A1%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A9_%D7%91%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99); [Boaz Mekler CPA](https://www.mekler.co.il/links/item/3139-%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%9C-%D7%A4%D7%A0%D7%A7%D7%A1%D7%99-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%E2%80%93-%D7%A1%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%95%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99.html)
- The taxpayer may scan in-house (no external scanning bureau required) if the process meets forgery-detection conditions. — [cpa.co.il](https://www.cpa.co.il/%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%9C-%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99/)
- Scope: **third-party documents only**: receipts, delivery notes, invoices, tax invoices, credit notes and orders. — [Payper](https://payper.co.il/marketing/article/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94_%D7%9C%D7%A1%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A9_%D7%91%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99)
- Archive duties include file backup, archive logs, and **technological continuity** when migrating systems. — [Goldpro CPA](https://www.goldpro.co.il/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%9B%D7%9F/%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94/%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%9C-%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A1%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%9E/)
- Software used to keep computerized books must be **registered with the ITA (רישום תוכנה לניהול מערכת חשבונות ממוחשבת)**, and the ITA publishes instructions for producing uniform-structure files as part of that service. — [gov.il, "instructions for producing uniform-structure files" (ITA service page PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/registration-software-designed-managing-computerized-accounting-system/he/Service_Pages_Income_tax_horaot-131.pdf); [Yehonatan Saadia, "registered software at the ITA"](https://yehonatandev.com/he/blog/tax-authority-registered-software-custom-system)

### Inferences
- A product that photographs or ingests supplier invoices can serve as the legal archive (letting the restaurant discard paper) only if it implements Appendix ו/ז requirements: the "מסמך סרוק" marking, an electronic signature (or two), an immutable archive with backup, and continuity. Otherwise it is a convenience copy, and the paper original must still be kept.
- Supplier invoices that arrive digitally (PDF via email from the supplier's registered software) are a different category from scans of paper. The scanning rules apply to paper third-party documents.

### Gaps
- Whether a **smartphone photo** satisfies the "computerized scanner" condition, and whether the ITA keeps a list of approved scanning software, were not confirmed.
- Retention period (commonly cited as 7 years) was not sourced in this session.
- Whether Appendices ו/ז were amended after 2013 was not confirmed.

## 4. Uniform structure file (מבנה אחיד), PCN874 detailed VAT reporting, and what the accountant needs monthly

### Takeaway
Registered accounting software must be able to export the ITA **uniform structure (מבנה אחיד / "Open Format")**: two text files, `INI.TXT` (summary) and `BKMVDATA.TXT` (data). Records include A000/A100 (headers), **C100 (document header), D110 (document lines), D120 (receipt/payment lines), B100 (journal entries), B110 (accounts), M100 (inventory items)** and Z900 (closing). Separately, the **PCN874 detailed VAT report (דיווח מפורט)** lists every input and output invoice individually, including the allocation number. From VAT periods beginning **September 2025** it is mandatory for dealers with annual turnover **above ₪500,000** or who must keep books.

### Cited Findings
- Uniform-structure output is two files: **INI.TXT** (lead/summary file with business and production-process details) and **BKMVDATA.TXT** (data). — [ITA guide "construction of single-format generation module", 01.08.2019 (gov.il PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/construction-single-format-generation-module/he/Guides_IncomeTax_Construction-single-format-generation-module-2019.pdf); [Rivhit, uniform-structure export PDF](https://www.rivhit.co.il/uploaded_files/documents/%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%90_%D7%A0%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%94_%D7%90%D7%97%D7%99%D7%93(4).pdf?fileid=515)
- Record codes: **B110** = accounts (trial-balance accounts), **B100** = journal entries, **C100** = document header, **D110** = document detail lines, **D120** = receipt/payment details, **M100** = inventory items, **Z900** = closing record with totals. INI.TXT opens with **A000**; BKMVDATA.TXT opens with **A100**. — [Karteset help, uniform format](https://karteset.co.il/help/uniform); [Tamit ERP help](https://tamit.co.il/erp/help/bkmv); [SAP Business One Israel docs](https://help.sap.com/saphelp_sbo900/helpdata/he/6e/f62bf5b2964484841b27c3be622b17/content.htm?no_cache=true)
- Current spec version cited by one vendor: **"הוראות ניהול פנקסים 1.31"**. The ITA PDF is titled "הוראות להפקת קבצים במבנה אחיד". — [Karteset](https://karteset.co.il/help/uniform); [gov.il ITA instructions PDF](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/registration-software-designed-managing-computerized-accounting-system/he/Service_Pages_Income_tax_horaot-131.pdf)
- **PCN874 expansion:** under ITA circular **27.2025**, detailed reporting is required for dealers with annual turnover above **₪500,000** or those required to keep books of account. It applies to VAT periods from **September 2025** onward, i.e. every report filed from **1 October 2025**. — [ZCPA, PCN874 in 2026](https://zcpa.co.il/Article/divuach-mforat-maam-2026); [Brit CPA, expansion of detailed reporting duty](https://britcpa.co.il/hozrim/%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%97%D7%91%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%92%D7%A9%D7%AA-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%9E%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%98-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%9E/)
- Deadline: the detailed report is filed online by the **23rd of the month following** the reporting period (monthly or bi-monthly reporters), in addition to the periodic VAT return. — [ZCPA](https://zcpa.co.il/Article/divuach-mforat-maam-2026); [Invoice4u, detailed VAT report](https://www.invoice4u.co.il/blog/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%9E%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%98-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%9E-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%97-874/)
- Content: each invoice separately: invoice number, date, amount before VAT, VAT amount, counterparty's VAT/business ID, and (where relevant) the **allocation number**. Submitted as a TXT file. The detailed report must fully reconcile with the periodic return. — [Bitan CPA, detailed VAT reporting guide](https://bitancpa.com/knowledge/detailed-vat-reporting-guide); [Linet](https://www.linet.org.il/%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%96%D7%94-pcn874-%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A3-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%A9%D7%AA-%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%9E-%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%91-2/); [ITA 2012 PCN change document (raf-cpa PDF)](https://www.raf-cpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/PCN.pdf); [Hashavshevet PCN874 help](https://home.wizcloud.co.il/help/pcn874/)

### Inferences
- Practically every restaurant with an עוסק מורשה file is above ₪500k turnover, so **all** its supplier invoices flow line-by-line into PCN874 each month. The ITA can therefore match the restaurant's claimed inputs against suppliers' outputs and against allocation numbers. A product that pre-validates each supplier invoice (VAT ID, amount, allocation number, 0%/18% split) before the accountant files reduces audit exposure.
- Monthly accountant needs (inferred from PCN874 fields + s.38 rules): every supplier tax invoice with the supplier's VAT ID, invoice number, date, pre-VAT amount, VAT amount and allocation number; credit invoices; and, for consolidated invoices, the delivery-note references. All of it is needed before the ~15th–23rd filing deadlines.
- Exporting purchase documents in C100/D110 shape would let a restaurant tool hand clean data to any registered accounting package.

### Gaps
- No official record-field specification (field positions/lengths for C100/D110) was read.
- The standard periodic VAT return deadline (commonly the 15th) vs the 23rd for online detailed filers was not separately confirmed.
- No source specifically listed "what an accountant needs monthly from a restaurant." The list above is inferred.

## 5. Supplier-payment law: "חוק מוסר תשלומים לספקים, התשע"ז-2017" (Payment Ethics law)

### Takeaway
The 2017 Payment Ethics law covers **private businesses too**, not just government. Secondary sources say private payers (עוסק מורשה, עוסק פטור, financial institutions) must pay **שוטף+45** by default, **unless the contract explicitly sets a different date**. Late amounts carry **linkage differences and interest**, and **late-payment interest (ריבית פיגורים)** after 30 days. Public bodies: government שוטף+30, local authorities שוטף+45, civil-engineering works שוטף+80.

### Cited Findings
- Law enacted **March 2017**. Private-sector rule: a business (including עוסק מורשה, עוסק פטור and financial institution) pays **שוטף + 45 days** unless the parties explicitly set another date in the contract. Without a written contract the statutory default is שוטף+45. — [Gatrimon law firm guide](https://www.gatrimon-law.co.il/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A8-%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%AA/); [Hon.co.il, "שוטף פלוס 30, 45, 60"](https://www.hon.co.il/%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%98%D7%A3-%D7%A4%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%A1-30-45-60-%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%90%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7/); [Davar, "שוטף +45 במרבית העסקאות במשק"](https://www.davar1.co.il/56567/)
- Public sector: state authorities, government ministries, the Sports Betting Council and Mifal HaPais pay **שוטף+30** (counted from the end of the month the invoice was submitted); local authorities **שוטף+45**; civil-engineering works **שוטף+80**. HMOs, public hospitals and MDA are excluded. — [Gatrimon](https://www.gatrimon-law.co.il/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A8-%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%AA/); [Chamber of Commerce, "אושר חוק שוטף פלוס שלושים"](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/legal/1858/75560/)
- Late payment: the unpaid amount carries **linkage differences and interest (הפרשי הצמדה וריבית)**, and after **30 days**, **ריבית פיגורים**. — [Kol Zchut, payment deadlines to suppliers](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%93_%D7%94%D7%90%D7%97%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9D_%D7%AA%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%94_%D7%9C%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8_%D7%A1%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%94_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA); [Gatrimon](https://www.gatrimon-law.co.il/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A8-%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%AA/)
- Historical/draft context (not the enacted text): Knesset committee drafts cited ריבית פיגורים "currently 6.5% a year" (rate date unknown). An amendment initiative cited שוטף+30 for public engineering works under ₪75,000 and שוטף+60 for government companies, statutory corporations and universities. — [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001247547); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local/articles/0,7340,L-3679788,00.html); [Small Business Agency (סבא"ה) policy page](https://www.sba.org.il/hb/PolicyAndInformation/Pages/k4.aspx)
- Conflict: sources disagree on whether the government rule is "45 days and no later than שוטף+30" or just שוטף+30, and some describe pre-enactment drafts. — [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%93_%D7%94%D7%90%D7%97%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9D_%D7%AA%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%94_%D7%9C%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8_%D7%A1%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%94_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA) vs [Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/legal/1858/75560/)

### Inferences
- For a private restaurant buying from a food supplier, the law is mostly a **default term**: if the supply contract is silent, the restaurant must pay by שוטף+45, and late-payment interest exposure applies. Because the parties can contract out, supplier price lists often specify their own terms (שוטף+30/60/90). A product can track each supplier's agreed terms vs the statutory default and flag both "paying too early" (cash-flow cost) and "paying late" (interest exposure).
- The statutory clock (end of month in which the invoice was delivered) aligns with consolidated month-end invoices, so due dates are computable from invoice metadata.

### Gaps
- The enacted statute text (sections on the private sector, any transaction-size or business-size limits, whether contractual terms are capped) was not read. Secondary sources do not mention a cap for private-to-private deals.
- No 2025–2026 amendment or active bill was found.
- The current statutory late-payment interest rate was not found.

## 6. E-invoicing / structured-invoice roadmap, and open banking / payment initiation (Bank of Israel)

### Takeaway
Israel's model is a **clearance (CTC) "allocation number" layer on top of ordinary invoices**: JSON data to the ITA, no mandated structured invoice format (no XML/UBL, no Peppol), and no official roadmap beyond the ₪5,000 threshold. On payments, Bank of Israel **Directive 368 (open banking)** and the payment-services / payment-initiation regime (ייזום תשלום) exist. Basic payment-initiation access obligations were rolling out around mid-2026. A **24 Sept 2026 draft amendment** adds cyber requirements for **advanced payment initiation**. Payment-company obligations were deferred by the 2026 Arrangements Law. Software that initiates supplier payments from a restaurant's bank account is legally foreseeable but still maturing.

### Cited Findings
**E-invoicing:**
- Sovos: "no official word" on Israel's e-invoicing plans beyond the current CTC (allocation-number) mandate. — [Sovos](https://sovos.com/vat/tax-rules/e-invoicing-israel/)
- Invoice data goes to the ITA in **JSON**; no digital electronic signature required; no XML requirement or Peppol link found. — [Flowie](https://www.get-flowie.com/e-invoicing/israel); [einvoicestudio](https://www.einvoicestudio.com/compliance-map/il); [Fonoa](https://www.fonoa.com/resources/blog/israel-introduces-e-invoicing-clearance-model)
- Vendor speculation about a future "zero threshold" (all tax invoices) has no date. — [BaseCRM](https://basecrm.co.il/guides/allocation-number-invoices-2026/)

**Open banking / payment initiation:**
- **Directive 368** ("Implementation of an Open Banking Standard in Israel") was published by the Banking Supervision Department in **February 2020**. Payment-initiation obligations were repeatedly deferred: a 2021 circular moved them to 31 Jan 2022, and circular C-06-2692 (Jan 2022) signalled further deferral of current-account payment-initiation access. — [BoI Directive 368 (EN)](https://kamakama.gov.il/media/1c1bdvxm/h2692_en.pdf); [BoI implementation doc (EN)](https://kamakama.gov.il/media/wwpckxlc/h2658_en.pdf); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/the-bank-of-israel-publishes-guidelines-for-implementation-of-the-open-banking-standard-in-israel)
- **June 2024** BoI amendments aligned Directive 368 with the payment-initiation law and widened scope to corporations controlled by supervised entities. — [BoI circular, 27 June 2024](https://www.boi.org.il/media/wpupcreo/111528.pdf)
- **April 2025** BoI circular: the payment-initiation regime requires giving access to a payment account for a **basic initiation service** and allowing payment orders through a dedicated interface, regardless of underlying technology. — [BoI circular, 20 April 2025](https://www.boi.org.il/media/crdevays/h2815.pdf)
- **21 June 2026** BoI Banking Supervision circular (h2851) on open banking (content not retrieved). — [BoI circular h2851](https://www.boi.org.il/media/0cgabv01/h2851.pdf)
- **7 June 2026**: Israel Securities Authority directive for **payment companies** (non-bank) on implementing the open-banking standard, covering basic and advanced initiation. It says the **2026 Economic Arrangements Law** deferred payment companies' open-banking obligations to **three years from license grant** (the earlier draft date was 6 June 2026). — [ISA directive PDF](https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-63a2db729e575/open_banking_forPSP.pdf); [Herzog Fox & Neeman](https://herzoglaw.co.il/en/news-and-insights/israel-securities-authority-publishes-directive-for-payment-companies-regarding-the-implementation-of-an-open-banking-standard/)
- **24 Sept 2026**: BoI published a **draft** amendment to Directive 368 with strict API cyber requirements for **advanced payment initiation**, leaving default transaction ceilings optional for users. The date label in the source is inconsistent, so verify it. — [IsraelDefense](https://www.israeldefense.co.il/en/node/71075)
- Overview of the regulated payment-services regime (licensing of payment / payment-initiation service providers) and 2025 outlook. — [Bar Law](https://barlaw.co.il/regulated-payment-services-and-financial-services-in-israel-summary-and-outlook-for-2025/)

### Inferences
- With no structured-invoice standard, restaurants will keep receiving PDFs, photos and paper. **OCR/extraction stays necessary**, and the ITA allocation-number API is the only national "structured" touchpoint (and only for the issuer side, as far as confirmed).
- Paying suppliers directly from software (pay-by-bank) would require either a licensed payment-initiation provider or a partnership with one. Bank-side basic initiation is becoming mandatory, while advanced initiation (likely needed for B2B batch payments) is still in draft rule-making as of Oct 2026. This is a 2027+ opportunity, not a launch feature.

### Gaps
- The exact go-live dates for bank-side basic and advanced payment initiation for **business** accounts were not confirmed.
- The name and number of the payment-initiation statute (likely the Regulation of Payment Services and Payment Initiation Law, 2023) were not confirmed from a primary source in this session.
- The Financial Information Service Law (account-information / open-finance licensing) was not researched.
- No source addressed Peppol or any ITA plan for a structured XML/UBL invoice.
