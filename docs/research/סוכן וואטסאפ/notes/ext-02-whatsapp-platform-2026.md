## 0. The seven findings that matter

1. **The AI ban does not target us, but Meta keeps sole discretion.** From **15 Jan 2026** the Business Solution Terms bar "AI Providers" from using the API when the AI is the *primary (rather than incidental or ancillary)* functionality being made available, "as determined by Meta in its sole discretion". Meta said businesses using AI for customer support, reservations or order tracking are unaffected. A vertical agent that only handles invoices, deliveries, orders and claims for staff who already use RestoMatch is the allowed case. A product pitched as "ask our AI anything on WhatsApp" is the risky case.
2. **The "free inside 24 h" premise is dead (now confirmed by Meta).** From **1 Oct 2026**, non-template service messages are charged per message after **1,000 free per business phone number per month**, and utility templates sent inside the window are charged again. Inbound stays free.
3. **The 1,000 free service messages are per *phone number*, not per customer.** If RestoMatch runs one number for all restaurants, every tenant shares one allowance. Budget for paid replies from about the third pilot restaurant.
4. **Israel unit costs are small but real.** Utility/authentication ≈ **$0.0053**, marketing ≈ **$0.0353** per delivered message (secondary sources, consistent across three; the official rate card was not reachable). The service rate equals the utility rate.
5. **A template that sounds promotional gets re-billed at ~6.7× the cost.** Since April 2025 Meta approves a "utility" template as marketing if it judges it so, and re-categorises live templates (often with one day's notice). One upsell line in a digest is enough.
6. **Groups are not a place for the agent.** The Cloud API Groups API (GA for Official Business Accounts since 3 Feb 2026) caps groups at **8 participants** and does **not support interactive messages**. One-to-one chats are the design.
7. **WhatsApp went down for 11–22 hours several times in 2026.** No T2 alert (₪ awaiting approval, a late truck) may depend on WhatsApp alone.

## 1. Meta's policy on AI on the Business API

**The clause.** Meta added an "AI Providers" section to the WhatsApp Business Solution Terms in October 2025, effective **15 January 2026**. As quoted by press from the terms: providers and developers of AI/ML technologies, "including but not limited to large language models, generative artificial intelligence platforms, general-purpose artificial intelligence assistants, or similar technologies as determined by Meta in its sole discretion", are prohibited from using the Business Solution, directly or indirectly, to provide those technologies "when such technologies are the primary (rather than incidental or ancillary) functionality being made available for use, as determined by Meta in its sole discretion". [press] TechCrunch, 18 Oct 2025, https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform ; MediaNama, Oct 2025, https://www.medianama.com/2025/10/223-whatsapp-bans-external-ai-providers-business-api/ (both seen 2026-11-07). I could not open the terms page itself (`whatsapp.com/legal/business-solution-terms`), so the wording is **as quoted by press**, not checked against the live text.

**What stays allowed.** Meta told press the change does not affect businesses using AI for customer support, reservations or order tracking. [press] same sources; respond.io, 2026, https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-policy-2026 (seen 2026-11-07): bots that serve a specific business function (FAQs, orders) remain allowed, and should offer a hand-off to a human or another channel.

**Enforcement and exceptions.**
- OpenAI, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot and others withdrew their WhatsApp bots around 15 Jan 2026. [press/secondary] Business Standard, Oct 2025, https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meta-bans-ai-chatbots-from-whatsapp-business-api-chatgpt-to-shut-down-jan-2026-125102200335_1.html (seen 2026-11-07).
- **Italy:** the competition authority (AGCM) imposed interim measures in **Dec 2025** suspending the terms; Meta excluded Italy. [press] GRC Report, https://www.grcreport.com/post/italian-antitrust-authority-orders-meta-to-halt-whatsapp-ai-terms-that-shut-out-rival-chatbots-2 ; AGCM press release https://en.agcm.it/en/media/press-releases/2025/12/A576 (seen 2026-11-07).
- **AI-provider fee:** Meta's own docs page "New pricing policy for AI Providers" says that from **16 Feb 2026**, in countries where Meta is legally required to support AI Providers, it charges them for non-template messages (a new `AI_BOT` pricing category), and "This does NOT change how Meta charges all other businesses". [Meta] https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/ai-providers/ (seen via search extract 2026-11-07). Press put the Italy rate at about $0.0691/message (UNVERIFIED against a rate card).
- **EU:** the Commission opened case AT.41034 on 4 Dec 2025, sent a Statement of Objections on 9 Feb 2026 and on **9 Jun 2026** ordered interim measures restoring access for rival general-purpose assistants; it treated the fee as equivalent to the ban. [official/press] https://europa.eu/newsroom/ecpc-failover/pdf/ip-26-1276_en.pdf ; https://ppc.land/eu-forces-meta-to-reopen-whatsapp-to-rival-ai-assistants/ (seen 2026-11-07). Whether the EU order covers Italy is disputed between sources (UNVERIFIED). **None of this applies to Israel**, where the ban stands as written.

**Meta's own agent.** Meta now sells a "Meta Business Agent", billed per token (**$2.00 per 1M tokens**, about 4–5 US cents per message by Meta's own example) from **1 Aug 2026**, charged even inside the free-entry-point window. [Meta] https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages (search extract, seen 2026-11-07). Replies generated by **our own** model (the latest Claude models) are ordinary "service messages from a human or third-party AI" in Meta's wording, billed at the service rate, which is ~10× cheaper per message than Meta's agent at Meta's example size. Press reports a further price gap against third-party AI on Meta's agent platform from 1 Oct (Outlook Business, https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/whatsapp-pricing-update-favours-meta-over-third-party-chatbots, seen 2026-11-07; figures UNVERIFIED).

**Human escalation.** The Business Messaging Policy has required since 2019 that automated chats offer "prompt, clear, and direct escalation paths" (in-chat transfer, phone, e-mail); a missing path can lower the quality rating. [secondary quoting the policy] Rasayel, https://learn.rasayel.io/en/books/whatsapp/whatsapp-business/whatsapp-business-messaging-policy ; Blip, https://help.blip.ai/hc/en-us/articles/4474389735191-Human-Escalation-Policy-in-WhatsApp-Business (seen 2026-11-07). I found **no** WhatsApp rule requiring that users be told they are talking to AI (UNVERIFIED either way; Israeli consumer or privacy law may differ, and that is a lawyer's question).

## 2. Templates, the 24-hour window and pricing

### 2.1 Categories and approval
- Three paid template categories: **marketing, utility, authentication**. Utility must be non-promotional and specific or essential to the user; mixed content (an order update with a promo, a survey with promotion) is marketing. [Meta] Template categorization, https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-categorization (search extract, seen 2026-11-07).
- **Since 9 Apr 2025** a template submitted as UTILITY that Meta judges MARKETING is *approved as marketing*; the `allow_category_change` flag was removed. Meta re-checks approved templates on a rolling basis and normally gives **1 day's notice** before switching a utility template to marketing (no notice for businesses already warned for misuse, from 16 Apr 2025). Appeals within **60 days**. The `template_category_update` webhook reports changes. [Meta] same page (seen 2026-11-07).
- **Marketing moves off Cloud API:** Cloud API stops supporting marketing messages on **13 Oct 2027**; marketing then goes through the Marketing Messages API. [Meta] https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/marketing-messages/overview (search extract, seen 2026-11-07). Utility templates reclassified as marketing must also route there.

### 2.2 The customer-service window and what is billed

## 4. Numbers, display name, verification, Cloud vs On-Prem, coexistence

- **Cloud API only.** New numbers can register only on Cloud API since 1 Jul 2024; the last On-Premises client expired **23 Oct 2025**, after which On-Prem cannot send. [Meta] https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/on-premises/sunset (seen 2026-11-07).
- **Display name and verification.** You can start sending before display-name review. **Business Verification** is the gate to scale and to request an Official Business Account; once verified, display-name review runs for all numbers. Common rejection reasons: a person's name, an unrelated business's name, a format problem, or a name inconsistent with the brand. [Meta] Business phone numbers / `phone_number_name_update` webhook / Facebook Business Help https://www.facebook.com/business/help/338047025165344 (seen 2026-11-07).
- **Official Business Account (blue tick)** criteria: complies with the policy, ≥ 30 days on the platform, verified portfolio, two-step verification on the number, approved display name. Non-OBA numbers do not appear in in-app search. [Meta] https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/official-business-accounts/ (page dated 19 Sep 2026 per extract; seen 2026-11-07).
- **Usernames:** from **29 Jun 2026** businesses can claim usernames; the display name is shown first when both exist. [Meta] changelog extract (seen 2026-11-07). Meta also documents "business-scoped user IDs" (https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-scoped-user-ids/); I could not read the details, so whether a user's phone number will always be present in webhooks is **UNVERIFIED**. Design implication: key staff identity on our own member record, and treat the WhatsApp id as one attribute.
- **Coexistence** (one number used in the WhatsApp Business app and on Cloud API at once): supported through Embedded Signup; vendors report fixed throughput (Telnyx: 20 mps), features disabled while linked (broadcast lists, edit/revoke, disappearing, view-once), the app must be opened at least every 14 days, and some regions excluded. [BSP] 360dialog https://docs.360dialog.com/partner/waba-management/whatsapp-coexistence ; Telnyx https://developers.telnyx.com/docs/messaging/whatsapp/coexistence.md ; seven.io https://help.seven.io/en/whatsapp/whatsapp-coexistence (seen 2026-11-07). A mid-2026 Meta forum thread reports mass portfolio restrictions hitting coexistence tech providers (anecdotal). https://developers.facebook.com/community/threads/891379180714484/

## 5. Quality, limits, bans and outages

- **Messaging limits** are now per **business portfolio** (shared by all its numbers) since **7 Oct 2025**, and count **unique users reached outside a customer-service window in a moving 24 h**. Ladder: **250 → 2,000 → 10,000 → 100,000 → unlimited**. 250 → 2,000 by completing Business Verification (or 2,000 high-quality out-of-window deliveries in 30 days, then Meta review). Above that, automatic: high quality plus using ≥ half the limit in 7 days raises one level within 6 h. [Meta] https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits ; …/upcoming-messaging-limits-changes/ (seen 2026-11-07).
- **Quality rating** is per number and per template. A number **Flagged for 7 days drops one limit level immediately**. Webhook `phone_number_quality_update`. Error **131048** = sending restricted because too many messages were blocked or reported; error **368** = account restricted/disabled for a policy violation (see Policy Enforcement + Health Status API). Blocked numbers can be appealed. [Meta] error codes https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/support/error-codes ; policy enforcement https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/policy-enforcement (seen 2026-11-07).
- **Per-user marketing limits** exist (Meta caps how many marketing templates one user receives); utility is not capped this way. https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/marketing-templates/per-user-limits/ (seen 2026-11-07; thresholds not shown in extract).
- **How to avoid bans:** message only opted-in people; keep templates strictly utility; honour opt-out at once; give a human escalation path; don't send what people block (frequency caps, quiet hours); watch quality webhooks and auto-pause a template whose quality drops. (Synthesis of the Meta pages above.) Meta also piloted a monthly cap on messages to people who never reply (Mobile Ecosystem Forum, 27 Oct 2025, https://mobileecosystemforum.com/2025/10/27/whatsapp-testing-spam-block-via-unread-messages-limit/; thresholds undisclosed).
- **Outages, 2026** (third-party trackers, durations approximate): Cloud API ~22 h from 6 Mar 2026 20:00 UTC; ~1.2 h on 30 Apr; webhook and media-send disruption ~11.7 h on 19 May; a global Meta outage on **12 Jun 2026** of ~11 h (SleekFlow) or ~16 h (IsDown). [secondary] https://isdown.app/status/meta/incidents/579331-outage-in-whatsapp-business-api-cloud-api ; https://isdown.app/status/sleekflow/incidents/605813-global-meta-outage-impacting-whatsapp-messenger-and-instagram (seen 2026-11-07). A 4 Aug 2026 report comes from a low-quality site (UNVERIFIED). Official history: metastatus.com (not checked).

## 7. Opt-in requirements

- **Meta:** before sending templates, obtain opt-in that (1) clearly states the person agrees to receive messages, (2) **names the business**, (3) complies with applicable law. Opt-in may be general, need not be WhatsApp-specific, and can be collected via web, SMS, IVR, in person or on paper. Recommended: separate opt-ins per category; a clear, honoured opt-out; watch quality when launching a new opt-in method. [Meta] https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in (seen 2026-11-07).
- **Israel, section 30A of the Communications Law (the "spam law")**: advertising messages need prior written consent; statutory damages up to **₪1,000 per message** without proof of harm. Exceptions exist (e.g. a one-time offer to a business asking whether it wants ads). Whether WhatsApp counts was reportedly settled by the Supreme Court in 2020 (consultant blog, **UNVERIFIED**); I found **no explicit carve-out for transactional messages**, though the law targets "advertisements". [secondary] Herzog/Meitar/law.co.il pieces, e.g. https://www.law.co.il/en/news/2016/08/19/israeli-anti-spam-law-amended-for-first-time/ ; https://achiya-automation.com/en/blog/whatsapp-marketing-spam-law-israel/ (seen 2026-11-07). **Lawyer to confirm.**

## 8. What this means for RestoMatch

All of this feeds **H-05** (delivery and scheduling: WhatsApp utility templates `rm_decision_needed`, `rm_delivery_late`, `rm_claim_reply`; role → member phone resolution at enqueue; quiet hours; ≤ 6 per person per day). `outbox.ts` and `cron.ts` are on the immutable list, so everything below is a **proposal for the human-authored H-05 PR**. WhatsApp credentials, Meta Business Verification, the payment method and the phone number are **Phase-6, human-only**.

**8.1 Position against the AI clause.**
- RestoMatch is a business (an invoice-leak SaaS) messaging **its own customers' staff** about **their** invoices, deliveries, orders and claims. AI is *ancillary* (it reads a photo or a voice note, drafts a reply). That is the case Meta says it leaves alone.
- Keep it obviously so. The agent answers only a closed set of intents (decisions, deliveries, orders, claims, "what's my leak"). Off-topic chat gets a fixed reply plus a link to the app. Never market it as "an AI assistant on WhatsApp". Always offer "דברו עם אדם" (the owner or RestoMatch support) to satisfy the escalation rule.
- Use our own model (the latest Claude models) behind the Cloud API, not Meta Business Agent. Our replies bill as service messages (≈ $0.0053 each in Israel), against ~4–5 US cents for a Meta-agent message at Meta's example size.
- Risk stays non-zero because Meta decides "primary" at its sole discretion. Mitigation: web push + e-mail stay first-class (see 8.5).

**8.2 Templates (utility only, per role).**
- One template set **per role**, so the role rules hold in the template text, not only in the app. The **receiver** templates carry **no ₪ variable at all**. The **chef** templates carry ₪ only for orders and recipes. The owner/manager carry ₪ for decisions and claims.
- No promo, upsell, "upgrade" or survey line in any utility template. One such line re-bills the template as marketing (≈ $0.0353 vs $0.0053, ~6.7×). Subscribe to `template_category_update` and alert a human when Meta flips one.
- The monthly report/digest send is the template most at risk of being judged marketing. Keep it factual (numbers + a link) and test it as a standalone template.
- No template ever goes to a **supplier**. Supplier contact stays the W2-06 hand-off (`wa.me` from the user's own WhatsApp, the user presses send). That keeps the API number clean of cold outbound and keeps "nothing sent to suppliers automatically".

**8.3 Money actions on WhatsApp.**
- A reply-button tap ("אשר") is a human action, but the WhatsApp session only proves phone possession. Proposal (owner decision needed): a button may *record intent*; the money effect needs a confirm step (a CTA URL into the app, or a second "אשר ₪X" reply naming the exact amount) unless an owner rule lowers it for small amounts. Use the 3-button limit for "אשר / השהה / פרטים".
- MISSING_ON_INVOICE stays out of any claim template and out of the leak figure in messages, the same as in the app. A claim reminder fires at day 7 ("overdue") as a **draft awaiting a tap**, never as a message to the supplier.

**8.4 Cost model (assumptions explicit).**
- Assumptions: one RestoMatch number for all tenants; Israel utility = service = $0.0053; FX ₪3.7/$ (**assumption**, not sourced here); 4 opted-in staff per restaurant.
- Out-of-window alerts (templates): the cap is 6 per person per day → at most 4 × 6 × 30 = 720/month per restaurant ≈ $3.8 ≈ ₪14 worst case. A realistic ~150/month ≈ $0.80 ≈ ₪3.
- In-window replies (service): free up to **1,000/month for the whole number**, shared by all tenants. Beyond that ≈ $0.0053 each. Example: 50 restaurants × 200 agent replies = 10,000/month → 9,000 paid ≈ $48 ≈ ₪176/month across the fleet.
- This is a **₪ cost line**, not leak. Never net it against the leak ₪ or add it to hours saved. It belongs in `docs/COSTS.md` (leak-economist's file).
- Put a payment method on the WhatsApp account before go-live. Without one, replies beyond 1,000 stop being delivered.

**8.5 Reliability and limits.**
- **Verify the business before the pilot.** An unverified portfolio may reach only **250 unique users per 24 h** outside a window. 60 restaurants × 4 staff already exceeds that.
- WhatsApp had 11–22-hour outages in 2026. Every T2 alert (₪ awaiting approval, a late truck, a blocking discrepancy) also goes to **web push** (W3 PWA) and, for owners, e-mail. The outbox records each channel separately, and mocks keep writing `skipped_no_provider`, never `sent`.
- Quiet hours 23:00–06:00 + Shabbat/holidays and ≤ 6 alerts/person/day also protect the quality rating (fewer blocks). Watch `phone_number_quality_update` and auto-pause a template on a drop.

**8.6 Media ingest (ocr-procurement-engineer's boundary).** Download every inbound photo, PDF or voice note **inside the webhook handler** (URL lives 5 min, id 7 days) into tenant-scoped storage, record the SHA-256, and send a ✅ reaction plus a typing indicator while OCR or transcription runs. A WhatsApp-forwarded invoice PDF may have lost its signature context: prefer the e-mailed original when one exists (earlier note). Voice notes are transcribed and the transcript is shown back before any write.

**8.7 Opt-in and identity.**
- Opt-in names **RestoMatch** (and the restaurant), is captured in-app at invite/onboarding into the planned `users.whatsapp_opt_in` (H-03), with a one-word opt-out ("עצור") honoured at once.
- Role → phone resolution keys on our member record, not on the WhatsApp id (business-scoped IDs and usernames are arriving).
- Staff alerts are not "advertising". Any growth/marketing WhatsApp (growth-pmm-engineer) needs prior written consent under section 30A and, from 13 Oct 2027, the Marketing Messages API.

**8.8 Shape.** One-to-one chats only in v1. Groups (≤ 8, OBA-only, no buttons) can't carry decision cards. Flows are a later option for the receiving checklist, after a security review of the encrypted endpoint. A **direct Cloud API** integration fits better than a BSP. If Meta onboarding stalls, 360dialog's flat per-number licence is the fallback. Avoid per-message-markup BSPs.
