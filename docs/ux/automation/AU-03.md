# AU-03 · Recovery chase loop v1: one overdue rule, a ready-to-send supplier reminder, a credit that asks to be confirmed
**One line:** found ₪ becomes returned ₪ more often. Every morning the 08:30 run gives the manager (and the bookkeeper, opt-in) one list of suppliers who are past 7 days. Each has a **pre-addressed** reminder draft: the supplier's own WhatsApp or e-mail is filled in, one tap to send. When a credit line shows up on a later invoice, a T2 row "אשרו זיכוי ₪X" proposes the oldest open claims to close; a human confirms.

Today:
- the e-mail and the home row disagree about who is overdue. `findOverdue` counts each claim incl. `claimed` and `acknowledged` (`reminders.ts:43-55`); `followUps` counts only `letter_sent`, per supplier by the latest letter (`recovery/logic.ts:186-220`);
- the letter opens `wa.me/?text=` with **no number** and `mailto:?` with **no address** (`letter-actions.tsx:44-45`), although `suppliers.contact_whatsapp/contact_email` exist;
- the bookkeeper never gets the reminder (`recipients.ts:28`);
- credit lines wait until someone opens the form.

Impact (catalogue A12, ASSUMPTIONS; the catalogue is authoritative and this line only points to it): **AU-03's own share ≈ ₪135–360/month recovered** (ASSUMED about half of the recovery bundle's gain). Context only: the whole bundle (W2-10 letters + this chase loop + AU-17 later) moves the collection share of the ₪1,800/month claimable (the part of the leak decided as a claim, not waived or disputed) from ~35% to ~65% ≈ ₪270–720/month, central ₪540 (35%→50% low, 35%→75% high; both shares ASSUMED, to be measured).
