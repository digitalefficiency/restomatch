# AU-06 · Ask answers "what needs me": waiting, stuck, owed, to pay, VAT at risk
**One line:** the owner, manager and (by decision) bookkeeper ask one Hebrew question and get the five operational answers they now assemble by clicking through four screens. Every ₪ comes from a read-only tool and is grounding-checked; nothing is written.
- **The five answers:** what waits for my decision · what is stuck in intake · who still owes me credits · what is due this week · how much input VAT is at risk.
- **Impact (assumption, research catalogue A11):** manager 0.25–0.5 h/week of navigation saved = ₪110–225/month time-₪ at ₪103/h loaded (labour value, not cash). Money-₪: none.
- **Today:** Ask has exactly four leak tools (`leak_hero`, `leak_report`, `leak_breakdown`, `claimable_lines`). "מה מחכה לי?" or "מי חייב לי כסף?" fall through to no intent. The bookkeeper, who handles claims and the pay run, cannot open Ask at all.
**Owner agent:** web-feature-engineer (leak-economist signs every ₪ label and the "unknown is never ₪0" copy; tenant-security-warden reviews the role table). **Device:** both. **Effort:** M (one PR; the bookkeeper switch is a separate commit that can be reverted alone). **humanPR:** no (read-only tools over existing read models; no schema, no `cron.ts`, no `outbox.ts`, no engine).
