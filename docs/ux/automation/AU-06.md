# AU-06 · Ask answers "what needs me"

**One line:** the owner and manager (and the bookkeeper, if approved) ask one Hebrew question and get the five operational answers they now assemble across four screens: what waits for my decision, what is stuck in intake, who still owes me credits, what to pay this week, and how much input VAT is at risk. Every ₪ comes from a read-only tool and is grounding-checked. Nothing is written except the KPI audit row.

Full research brief: `docs/ux/automation/AU-06.md` on `origin/claude/research-in-app-automation`. This file is the build brief (kickoff 7.11 shape).
