# AU-08 · Allocation-number guard and chase: keep the 18% input VAT deductible
**One line:** an invoice above the allocation threshold (₪5,000 before VAT from 1.6.2026) without a valid 9-digit מספר הקצאה becomes a tracked case that ends. This ₪ is **"מע״מ בסיכון", shown apart from the leak and never added to it.**
- **The case:** a pre-addressed request to the supplier in one tap → "נשלחה בקשה לפני 4 ימים" → a typed or copied number that closes it; an ITA verification link with the fields ready to copy; a countdown to the 6-month deduction deadline.
- **Impact (assumption):** 0–1 such invoice a month at a ₪200k/month restaurant. One miss on a ₪6,000 invoice = ₪1,080 VAT not deductible, so the full exposure is ₪0–1,100/month, lumpy (most months ₪0); AU-08's own increment is **≈₪0–550/month VAT kept** (catalogue A13: the full ₪ is shared with the VAT-at-risk card and the W2-03 hold). Time: 0–15 min/month = 0–0.058 h/week ≈ **₪0–22/month time-₪** at ₪89 (catalogue A13; bookkeeper/owner, labour value; ₪0–26 at the owner's ₪103). Never summed with each other.
- **Today:** the flag computes the risk (lab: ₪1,123.20 at risk) and offers a Hebrew text to copy. No screen can store the number once the supplier sends it (`updateInvoiceHeader` has no such field), nothing records that a request was sent, nothing counts down, and nobody is reminded.
**Owner agent:** web-feature-engineer (leak-economist signs that VAT ₪ never reaches the leak, the hero, letters or the pay-run credit; tenant-security-warden reviews the new mutations). **Device:** both (cockpit field and card on desktop; the request hand-off is a phone action). **Effort:** M (one PR; UI slices wait for W2-02 / W2-08 merges). **humanPR:** no (column exists; no schema, no engine, no `cron.ts`: the daily deadline slot is the H-05 addendum line already proposed).
