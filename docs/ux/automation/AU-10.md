# AU-10 · Intake autopilot 2: trusted senders proposed, one approval per sender, cross-channel copies held, the bookkeeper can upload
**One line:** the inbox stops asking the same question twice:
- a supplier whose WhatsApp number or e-mail is already on its card, or an unknown sender the manager has approved twice, is **proposed** for the allow-list. One tap, never automatic;
- several documents waiting from one sender are approved together;
- the same paper arriving as a WhatsApp photo **and** an e-mailed PDF is held as a neutral (T4) "possible copy" before it is matched or paid;
- the bookkeeper can finally upload an invoice that came with no delivery.

Today:
- the allow-list is typed one sender at a time (4 fields), re-typing what the supplier card holds (`routers/ingest.ts:196-226`, `schema.ts:433-434`);
- every unknown-sender document is 2-3 taps, one by one (`inbox-client.tsx:296-366`);
- a cross-channel copy differs in bytes and object, so it is caught only by number, and only when both rows carry a supplier (int-01 §6);
- `receiving.registerInvoice` requires a `grId` (`receiving.ts:1013-1016`), so a PDF from a portal or forwarded mail has no way in.

Hours (spec §1 and catalogue A15, ASSUMPTIONS): manager 0.08–0.3 h/week (20–40 inbox documents × 1–2 min of triage) and bookkeeper 0.1–0.25 h/week (no re-typing of PO-less invoices elsewhere). That is **₪75–230/month hours-₪** at ₪89–103/h. The bookkeeper part is labour value, and cash falls only if a per-document fee falls. ₪ protected: a cross-channel copy is held at intake instead of at payment. That ₪ is counted once, under AU-14 (A19). It is never leak ₪.

**Owner agent:** ocr-procurement-engineer (tenant-security-warden reviews the new mutations, the allow-list path and the upload path; leak-economist signs that nothing here moves leak ₪). **Device:** both (inbox on desktop and phone). **Effort:** M. Ship as 3 PRs:
- a = sender proposals + approve-all-from-sender;
- b = cross-channel copy hold (+ the one hook in `ocrInvoice.ts`);
- c = bookkeeper upload.

**humanPR:** no. `schema.ts`, `cron.ts`, `outbox.ts` and `packages/matching/**` are untouched, and no new `DiscrepancyType` is added.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Sender proposals (A15a).** `senderProposals(db, rid, now)` returns at most 10 proposals, newest evidence first. Two bases:
   - `supplier_contact`: an active supplier whose `normalizeSender('whatsapp', contact_whatsapp)` or `normalizeSender('email', contact_email)` has no `ingest_senders` row of that kind, and whose channel of that kind is on;
   - `approved_twice`: a sender not on the allow-list with ≥2 documents a human approved (`ingest_documents.status = 'registered'` from a message with `sender_allowed = false`) in the last 90 days, all registered to the **same** supplier. Mixed suppliers → no proposal.
   A proposal dismissed in the last 90 days (audit `ingest.sender_proposal_dismissed` with the same `kind:sender`) is not shown.
   - `ingest.senderProposals` (finance read); `ingest.acceptSenderProposal({kind, sender, supplierId, basis, clientMutationId})` (manager; it inserts through the `addSender` statement and re-checks the basis server-side, so a stale proposal → CONFLICT); `ingest.dismissSenderProposal({kind, sender})` (manager).
   - Copy: "לסמוך על 054-123-4567? · זה המספר של תנובה בכרטיס הספק · מסמכים ממנו ייקראו אוטומטית · [הוסיפו לרשימה] [לא עכשיו]"; and "אישרתם 2 מסמכים מ-orders@x.co.il (שויכו לשטראוס) · לסמוך עליו מעכשיו?". For e-mail the card adds: "דורש אימות שולח (DMARC) — כמו היום".
2. **Approve all from one sender (A15b).** The inbox groups `needs_review` documents by normalised sender. A group with ≥2 shows "אשרו את כל 3 המסמכים מ<שולח>" and an "וגם הוסיפו לרשימה" checkbox, ticked only when the group has a single supplier.
   - It calls W2-02's `ingest.bulkApprove` (or your `ingest.approveMany({items:[{documentId, expectedStatus:'needs_review'}], supplierId?, allowSender, clientMutationId})`, max 50).
   - All-or-nothing with per-item CONFLICT. Over-quota documents (no `storage_path`) are left out and named: "2 מסמכים ללא קובץ · בקשו מהשולח לשלוח שוב".
   - One audit row per document (`ingest.bulk_approved`, `automationId: 'A15'`). A replay registers nothing new.
3. **Cross-channel copy hold (A15c).** `crossChannelCandidate(db, rid, invoice)` returns the first other invoice of this restaurant with:
   - the same non-null `supplier_id`;
   - `|total_incl_vat − other.total_incl_vat| ≤ ₪1.00`, compared in integer agorot;
   - `invoice_date` within ±2 Israel days;
   - a different `source`, **or** the same source with a different ingest message and a different sha256;
   - a different or missing `invoice_number` (an equal number is already the unique index's job: skip);
   - the other row not `rejected` and not itself marked a copy.
   On a hit, `crossChannelHold()` (called from `ocrInvoice.ts` after the header write, before the match chain) merges `raw.possibleDuplicateOf = {invoiceId, source, invoiceDate, totalInclVat}` plus the review reason `possible_duplicate` into `ocr_payload`, keeps the invoice in review, and **never enqueues `match-invoice`**. Three details make the hold real:
   - **The guard is the post-header state.** By the time the hook runs, `writeOcrHeader` has already set the status to `parsed` or `matched` (`result.needsHumanReview ? 'parsed' : 'matched'`, `ocrInvoice.ts:270-292`), so a merge guarded on `status = 'ocr_pending'` would match no row. Guard on `id` + `restaurant_id` + `status IN ('parsed','matched')`, and set `status = 'parsed'` **in the same UPDATE**;
   - **Feed the job's own decision.** Fold the hit into `needsHumanReview` (`ocrInvoice.ts:294`) before the auto-match decision (`:334-336`), so the job's chain does not enqueue;
   - **Close the second auto-match path.** The receipt-submit trigger auto-matches invoices with `status = 'matched'` and no match run, and skips only `raw.duplicateOf`, not `raw.possibleDuplicateOf` (`packages/api/src/receiving/matchTriggers.ts:234-251`, `:313-314`). Because the held copy is now `parsed`, that query never selects it; AU-10 does not edit `matchTriggers.ts` (W2-05/W3-09's). If the orchestrator prefers belt and braces, a one-line `possibleDuplicateOf` skip there needs that path in the boundary and its owner's OK.
4. **The copy chip and verdict.** On the inbox row and the invoice page (finance only), T4 neutral: "ייתכן שזו אותה חשבונית שהגיעה במייל ב-12.11 (₪4,513.15) · [זו אותה חשבונית] [חשבונית נפרדת]".
   - `ingest.resolvePossibleDuplicate({invoiceId, verdict: 'same'|'separate', clientMutationId})` (`can('intake.approve')`; interim manager + bookkeeper).
   - `separate`: removes the marker, audit `ingest.copy_resolved` (`verdict`, A15), and enqueues the match if no other review reason remains (same rule as AU-02's `ocrMayAutoMatch`, moved into `packages/api/src/ingest/autoMatch.ts` by AU-02).
   - `same`: writes `raw.duplicateOf = {invoiceId, invoiceNumber, basis: 'cross_channel'}` where `invoiceNumber` = the twin's number (or this row's read number when the twin has none). That keeps the existing shape `writeOcrHeader` writes (`{invoiceId, invoiceNumber}`, `ocrInvoice.ts:456-462`), which the readers rely on: `buildMatchInput` raises `DUPLICATE_INVOICE` from `duplicateOf.invoiceNumber` when a person runs the match (`match/buildMatchInput.ts:136-145`) and receiving shows `duplicateOfNumber` (`routers/receiving.ts:454`). It keeps the row out of matching and audits it. It is never paid as a separate invoice: W2-03 lists checked invoices only, and AU-14 holds a `raw.duplicateOf` row as "עותק · לא לשלם".
   - A stale marker (the other invoice rejected since) → CONFLICT naming it.
5. **Bookkeeper upload (A15d).** An "העלו חשבונית" button on the inbox for owner, manager and bookkeeper (and only for them).
   - Upload through `scans.upload` (multi-page through W2-05 `scans.composeDocument` once merged), then `ingest.registerLooseInvoice({documentRef, supplierId | null, clientMutationId})` (`can('intake.approve')`; interim `financeProcedure`).
   - It checks the object ref is this tenant's scan path (`isAllowedScanUrl` / G-09 `toObjectRef`), dedupes per (restaurant, object) under the advisory lock exactly like `registerInvoice`, inserts the invoice through G-05's create path with `source = 'manual'` and `status = 'ocr_pending'`, and enqueues OCR after commit.
   - With no supplier, AU-02's suggestion runs on the read. With OCR mode `disabled` for the tenant, the row fails as `ocr_disabled` and the page says "הקריאה האוטומטית כבויה למסעדה · הזינו ידנית בעמוד החשבונית".
   - It doesn't need the `ingestWhatsapp`/`ingestEmail` flags (no `requireIngest`). Audit `ingest.loose_registered` (A15).
6. **Server enforcement and tenancy.** Every new procedure is restaurant-scoped. A foreign `documentId`, `invoiceId`, `supplierId` or object ref → NOT_FOUND, and B is untouched. Receiver and chef → FORBIDDEN on every new procedure, and the inbox route stays closed to them. No proposal ever reads another tenant's suppliers or senders.
7. **No ₪ moved.** The lab hero (₪759.54), the pay run and the letters are identical before and after on the seed. A held copy carries no leak ₪, and its chip ₪ is the invoice total for finance roles only.
8. **KPI hook (AU-07).** Audit actions `ingest.sender_proposal_accepted`, `ingest.sender_proposal_dismissed`, `ingest.bulk_approved`, `ingest.copy_held`, `ingest.copy_resolved`, `ingest.loose_registered`, each with `automationId: 'A15'` and the basis/verdict in `after`.
9. **Off switch.** With an option off: no proposal card, no group action, no hold (the OCR path behaves as today) and no upload button, and the procedures return FORBIDDEN `OPTION_OFF`. `FEATURES_OFF` doesn't apply (options), and AU-01's page lists the three rows.
