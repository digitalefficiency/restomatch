# AU-11 · Order autopilot: the draft is ready before the cut-off, the missing goods are re-ordered, a failed send is rescued
**One line:** on an order day the chef or manager opens a **prepared draft** ("טיוטה מוכנה · 9 פריטים") built from the W2-06 order guide at the restaurant's own expected prices, edits it, and sends it. Nothing is ever sent by the system.
- When a delivery closes short, a **backorder draft** with the missing quantities is waiting.
- When a PO send failed or had no provider, the orderer hears about it and finishes the send from their own WhatsApp in one tap.

Today:
- the 09:00 job only says "לא בוצעה הזמנה" and treats an unsent draft as ordered (`orderReminders.ts:81-97`; AU-04 fixes the truth);
- the chef re-builds the same weekly list (int-05 A1);
- a short delivery leaves nothing prepared (`receiving.ts:985` sets `partial` and stops);
- a failed send is visible only to someone who reopens the PO page (`orders.ts:324-334` `sendStateOf`, polled by the page).

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Prepared draft (A16a).** `prepareOrderDraft(db, rid, supplierId, orderDay)`:
   - lines = W2-06 `orderGuide` rows with a `usualQty` (median of the last 4), at most 40;
   - prices from `resolveExpectedPrice` (contract > last explicit > list) with `asOf`; a line with no expected price keeps a null price;
   - it inserts one PO `status 'draft'`, `source 'recurring'`, `source_ref 'au:A16:<supplierId>:<orderDay YYYY-MM-DD>'`, `created_by NULL`, `notes 'טיוטה מוכנה לפי מדריך ההזמנה'`, under `pg_advisory_xact_lock(<rid>:<source_ref>)` plus a select-by-`source_ref` (the `captureOrderDraft` pattern; the unique index doesn't fire on NULL platform);
   - it is skipped when the supplier already has a non-cancelled PO created today, an open human draft, fewer than 2 guide rows, or is inactive;
   - it **never** changes any PO's status and never enqueues an outbox row (test).
2. **The 09:00 message.** For each supplier due today with option `preparedOrderDraft` on, after the draft exists: "טיוטה מוכנה ל<ספק> · 9 פריטים · השליחה עד 14:00 · [פתחו את הטיוטה]".
   - Deep link `/dashboard/go?r=<rid>&to=/dashboard/orders/<poId>`. Recipients: owner, manager and chef through `notify()`.
   - **No ₪** in the message, even though the chef may see ₪ on the screen.
   - Dedupe `au:A16:<rid>:<userId>:<supplierId>:<ilDate>`. For that supplier it replaces AU-04's A5 line: one message per supplier per person per slot (test).
   - A perishable skip as AU-04 §5.7: past the same day's cut-off → no message.
3. **Adoption.** `openDraft({supplierId})` (W2-06's) returns the prepared draft to the first orderer who opens it. A guarded `UPDATE … SET created_by = :me WHERE id = :po AND created_by IS NULL` makes it theirs, and audit `order.prepared_draft_opened` (A16).
   - A second orderer then sees "פתוחה אצל <שם>" (W2-06's one-draft rule).
   - `orders.preparedDrafts()` (`chefProcedure`, `redactPrices` for anyone without price view) feeds a T4 banner on the board: "טיוטות מוכנות: תנובה · שטראוס · [לבדיקה ושליחה]".
   - `orders.dismissPreparedDraft({poId, clientMutationId})` cancels an unopened prepared draft (`draft → cancelled` through G-05's guard), audit A16 `dismissed`. A sent PO → CONFLICT.
4. **Sending stays human.** Only `placeOrder` / `markSentManually` / `sendMany` (W2-06) move a prepared draft. The detail page says "הוכנה אוטומטית · בדקו כמויות לפני שליחה" until the first edit.
5. **Backorder draft (A16b).** On `receipt.closed_with_exceptions` with `status = 'partial'`, for each PO line with `qty_received < qty_ordered`:
   - the guard is on units **billed but not received**, never on `QTY_SHORT`. In the engine, missing units the supplier DID bill raise `QTY_OVER` (billed > received, severity block, `deltaAmount = invPrice × billedNotReceived`, `engine.ts:247-252, 294-307`); `QTY_SHORT` keeps only the shortfall the supplier did **not** bill and drops to ₪0 when the invoice billed every missing unit (`engine.ts:258-266`; the ₪0 "twin" noted at `routers/payRun.ts:146-148`). So a `QTY_SHORT` with ₪ > 0 marks exactly the units that **should** be re-ordered;
   - billed-not-received units per line = the units of an open or accepted `QTY_OVER` on that `po_line`/`gr_line`, or, when an invoice is linked but not yet matched, `max(0, invoice qty_billed − qty_received)` on that line;
   - re-order qty = ordered − **max(received, billed)**. A line whose missing units were all billed is excluded (they become a credit claim, not a re-order), and the draft notes list it: "<מוצר>: לא נוסף · החשבונית כבר מחייבת את הכמות החסרה (דרישת זיכוי)"; a partly billed line is reduced and noted the same way;
   - **no invoice linked yet** when the event fires (common: `receipt.closed_with_exceptions` usually comes before the invoice is matched): the draft is still prepared with ordered − received, and its notes and banner say "החשבונית עוד לא הותאמה · בדקו שהספק לא חייב על הכמות החסרה לפני שליחה". The human sends; nothing is sent automatically;
   - priced by `resolveExpectedPrice`;
   - one draft `source 'manual'`, `source_ref 'au:A16b:<grId>'`, `created_by NULL`, `notes 'השלמה להזמנה #<ref>'`, delivery date from the supplier's order schedule. Idempotent per GR (event replay → one draft);
   - `notify()` to the PO's creator + managers: "חסרו 2 פריטים מ<ספק> · הכנו טיוטת השלמה · [פתחו]". No ₪, never to receivers. Dedupe `au:A16b:<rid>:<userId>:<grId>`;
   - nothing when every short line is excluded.
6. **Failed-send rescue (A16c).** At the 09:00 run, POs placed in the last 48 h whose `po:<id>:placed` outbox row is `failed` or `SKIPPED_NO_PROVIDER` (via `sendStateOf`), not `markSentManually`'d and with no goods receipt:
   - `notify()` to the PO creator (else managers): "ההזמנה ל<ספק> לא נשלחה · שלחו מהוואטסאפ שלכם בלחיצה";
   - the link opens the PO, where W2-06's "מהוואטסאפ שלי" sends;
   - dedupe `au:A16c:<rid>:<userId>:<poId>`. It reads the outbox only and never retries it (that is `outboxDispatch`, H-05).
7. **Roles and ₪.** Owner, manager and chef only. The receiver gets FORBIDDEN on the new procedures, no notification and no banner. Every message body is ₪-free (test: no `₪`, no 2-decimal number).
8. **Server enforcement and tenancy.** Every query is restaurant-scoped. A foreign `poId` → NOT_FOUND. The event handler re-reads the GR under the event's restaurant id, never trusting the payload.
9. **KPI hook and off switch.** Audit `order.prepared_draft_created|opened|dismissed` (A16), `order.backorder_draft_created` (A16), outbox prefixes `au:A16:`, `au:A16b:`, `au:A16c:`; AU-07 reads "prepared drafts sent within the day %". Each of the three options off → the job logs `skipped: option off` and creates nothing (test both states).

Where the docs conflict: the spec says "push prepared text" for a failed send. With no real push before H-05, it goes through `notify()` (e-mail + in-app bell), and the push follows when H-05 lands.
