# AU-12 · Nothing waits silently: decision SLA nudges and the bookkeeper's weekly digest
**One line:** a routed gap that nobody has decided for 24 hours gets **one** nudge to the person it waits for. At 48 hours the owner is **notified** who is holding it; `requires_role` never changes, so who approves stays the same. Every Sunday the bookkeeper gets one message with the week's work:
- what is due to pay, and what is on hold and why, with ₪ in dispute;
- VAT at risk, with the nearest deadline;
- overdue credit claims;
- what has not been exported.

Today:
- no job reminds anyone of an undecided gap (int-03 §5: "No scheduled nudges for approvals");
- the bookkeeper receives nothing from any scheduled job (`recipients.ts` defaults to owner + manager, int-07 §3.2);
- the four lists live on four screens.

Hours (catalogue A17, ASSUMPTIONS, trimmed): bookkeeper 0.25–0.5 h/week (15–30 min a week of assembling four screens; the pay-run and recovery pages already group part of it) ≈ **₪95–195/month hours-₪** at ₪89/h. That is labour value inside the bookkeeper cap (catalogue §0: her total time-₪ across all AU items is never shown above her monthly fee); cash falls only if the bookkeeper's fee falls. Money-₪ is **not counted**: the nudges protect the same claimable pool as AU-03 (a gap decided before payment can be offset instead of claimed). Illustration only: ≈₪100–250/month of the ₪1,800 claimable pool, never added to AU-03's number.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **SLA candidates.** `slaCandidates(db, rid, now)` returns open routed items (G-12 `awaitingDecision` scope) with `{discrepancyId, invoiceId, supplierName, routedRole, ageHours, claimableIls}`. The age comes from G-12's definition (the gap's first routing, carried across re-matches), never a re-match row's `created_at`. Items in `toReview` (no ₪) count too, with ₪ omitted.
2. **Nudge at 24 h (in the 19:00 message).** For each decider whose routed items are older than 24 h and **not nudged before**, their evening message gets one section:
   - "ממתינים לך יותר מיום: 3 פערים · ₪296.00 · הוותיק 4 ימים";
   - then up to 3 lines, oldest first: "<ספק> · חשבונית <מס׳> · ₪100.00 · [החליטו]", each deep-linked `/dashboard/go?r=<rid>&to=/dashboard/approvals?invoice=<id>`.
   One audit row per item per level (`approvals.sla_nudged`, `after: {level: 24, automationId: 'A17'}`) makes it "once". Re-routing by the owner (`escalate`) starts a new 24 h clock for the new role.
3. **Owner notice at 48 h.** In the owner's evening message: "ממתינים יותר מיומיים אצל: מנהל · 2 פערים · ₪180.00 · מנהלת חשבונות · 1 · ₪95.00". Once per item (audit level 48). Items routed to the owner get only the 24 h nudge. **No `requires_role` change, no escalate call, no auto-decision** (test: every `discrepancies` row is byte-identical before and after the job).
4. **One message per slot.** The sections live inside AU-04's single evening message per person. A person with only a nudge and an otherwise quiet day still gets one message. Sections never repeat what AU-04's lines already say: the count line is AU-04's, the per-item lines are yours. Friday 19:00 defers to Saturday 21:00 (G-06); the nudge is not perishable.
5. **Weekly digest builder.** `weeklyDigest(data, locale)` is pure, takes `weeklyData(db, rid, weekStartIlDate)` and returns `{subject, text, html, deepLink, dedupeSuffix}`. Sections, each omitted when its source is missing or zero:

   - "לא יוצאו להנהלת חשבונות: 23 חשבוניות מאז 01.11" (H-03 `export_runs`; omitted until then).
   Each section links to its screen through go-links. ₪ only via `moneyText`. Never "דליפה", never "1.5%", never an hours estimate.
6. **Weekly send.**
   - Interim: the Sunday 19:00 run of `endOfDayReport.ts` sends the weekly digest to each opted-in bookkeeper **instead of** that bookkeeper's evening message (the evening lines are appended as the last section), dedupe `au:A17w:<rid>:<userId>:<weekStartIlDate>`.
