# AU-13 · Prices that went up, in ₪ per month: one morning mail instead of one per alert, plus contracts ending and dishes crossing their target
**One line:** the 07:00 price sweep (already scheduled; a no-op on every pilot tenant until AU-01 turns `priceAlerts` on) sends each owner and manager **one** "מחירים שעלו" mail. It ranks every new rise by its estimated **₪ per month** ("≈₪212 בחודש (הערכה)", W2-12's helper), and the same mail says which contract prices end within 14 days. The chef gets the dishes whose food cost crossed the target this month, with ₪ per plate (₪ is allowed on recipes).
- A new supplier price list adds "12 מחירים עלו" to that mail once the catalog import records its diff.
- None of it is leak ₪, and none of it writes `price_history` (AD11).
