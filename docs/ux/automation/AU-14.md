# AU-14 · Pay-run pre-flight: check before the money leaves
**One line:** when the bookkeeper opens the pay run, every invoice about to be paid is checked for four risks:
- a possible duplicate across months and channels;
- a supplier's first payment;
- bank details changed since the last payment: a **hard hold that only the owner releases**, after a call to the supplier;
- an unusual amount.

Each flag carries its reason and the ₪ at stake. Every supplier also gets a ready-to-send payment note that names what was offset and what is still in review, as a draft the human sends.

Today:
- the pay run is a read-only report;
- only a same-number duplicate is held (`payRun.ts:138-142`);
- bank details don't exist yet (H-03 adds `supplier_bank_accounts` + `_changes`);
- nothing marks an invoice paid (W2-03 adds `markPaid`).

Impact (catalogue A19, ASSUMPTIONS):
- bookkeeper 0.25–0.5 h/week ≈ **₪95–195/month hours-₪** (labour value);
- money-₪, a wrong payment stopped: **unknown until AU-07 measures it** (catalogue §F row 5). Context only: APQC puts 0.8–2% of payments by count at risk [cross-industry]; at ~60 invoices/month that is 0.5–1.2 events, and AU-14's increment over the engine and W2-03 holds is ASSUMED to be 10–30%, so ≈₪3,333 gross per event × 0.05–0.36 events/month. A payment held is not a loss avoided: most wrong payments to a known supplier come back by offset or refund;
- a bank-detail fraud costs a whole payment and is kept out of the expectation;
- never leak ₪, never summed with hours-₪.

**Owner agent:** web-feature-engineer (tenant-security-warden **mandatory**: the owner-only release and the bank path; leak-economist signs the flag ₪ and the remittance wording). **Device:** both (bookkeeper desktop, owner release on the phone). **Effort:** M. Ship as 2 PRs:

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **The rules (`payRun/preflight.ts`, pure).** For each unpaid checked invoice of the restaurant:
   - `possible_duplicate` (soft): another invoice of the same supplier, any month, any channel, with `|Δ total_incl_vat| ≤ ₪1.00` (integer agorot) **or** ≤0.5% of the larger, and `invoice_date` within ±3 Israel days. Excluded: the same row, a pair already linked by `DUPLICATE_INVOICE` or `raw.duplicateOf`, a pair covered by G-04 `note_billed_twice`, and credit notes (negative totals). Text: "ייתכן כפל: דומה ל-MF-24950 מ-28.10 (₪7,363.20, נשלחה בוואטסאפ)";
   - `confirmed_copy` (hold, not selectable, like W2-03's duplicate): `raw.duplicateOf.basis = 'cross_channel'` (AU-10 verdict). Text: "עותק של חשבונית ששולמה/תשולם · לא לשלם";
   - `first_payment` (soft): no invoice of this supplier has `paid_at` (H-03) and none is `paid`. Text: "תשלום ראשון לספק · ודאו פרטי בנק";
   - `bank_changed` (**hard**): a `supplier_bank_account_changes` row for the supplier with `changed_at` after the supplier's last `paid_at` (or in the last 90 days when never paid), with no owner confirmation after it (§5.3). Text: "פרטי הבנק של שטראוס השתנו ב-03.12 (חשבון ‎…4821) · אמתו בטלפון מול הספק לפני תשלום";
   - `unusual_amount` (soft): `total_incl_vat` > 2 × the median of the supplier's last 12 checked invoices, with ≥3 samples. Text: "סכום חריג: פי 2.4 מהרגיל לספק (חציון ₪3,050.00)".
   Each flag returns `{kind, severity: 'soft'|'hard'|'hold', reasonKey, vars, atRiskIls}` where `atRiskIls` = the invoice total incl. VAT.
2. **`payRun.preflight({invoiceIds?})`** (finance tier: owner, manager, bookkeeper). One batched read (no N+1; ≤3 queries for 500 invoices) returns flags per invoice plus counts per kind. It is read-only: no audit row, no state change. Receiver and chef → FORBIDDEN.
3. **Owner release of a bank change.** `payRun.confirmBankChange({supplierId, changeId, method: 'phone_callback'|'in_person'|'other', note, clientMutationId})`, **`ownerProcedure` only** (G-07 `can('payrun.release')` = owner later):
   - it checks that the change row is this restaurant's and is the latest for the supplier, else CONFLICT naming the newer change;
   - it writes one audit row `payrun.bank_change_confirmed` (`after: {changeId, method, note, automationId: 'A19'}`) that the rule reads as the confirmation. If W2-11 ships a verify mutation that sets `supplier_bank_accounts.verified_at`, call that instead and still audit. Never both;
   - a replay writes nothing new;
   - manager and bookkeeper → FORBIDDEN (attack test).
   The confirm dialog requires the method and reads: "אישרתי מול הספק בטלפון שמספרו בכרטיס הספק (לא מהחשבונית או מהמייל)".
4. **The `markPaid` guard.** W2-03's `markPaid` calls `assertPreflightClear(db, rid, invoiceIds, acknowledgedFlags)`:
   - an invoice with an unconfirmed `bank_changed` or a `confirmed_copy` → PRECONDITION_FAILED `{reason: 'preflight_hold', kind}`, with nothing written for any item (all-or-nothing, as W2-03);
   - an invoice with soft flags needs every soft flag's kind in `acknowledgedFlags`, else PRECONDITION_FAILED `{reason: 'preflight_unacknowledged'}`;
   - the audit row of each paid invoice records the acknowledged kinds (`automationId: 'A19'`).
5. **The panel.** Above the pay-run table: "בדיקה לפני תשלום · 3 דגלים · ₪14,212.40 בבדיקה".
   - Hard and hold rows first: T2 danger badge + ₪ + one CTA.
     - The owner sees "אמתו ואשרו".
     - Others see "ממתין לאישור הבעלים".
     - Hard rows can't be selected in W2-03's BatchBar (the row shows the reason as text).
   - Soft rows: warn chips on the table row, and the BatchBar's confirm dialog lists them with one checkbox each: "בדקתי: ייתכן כפל עם MF-24950".
   - Zero flags → one calm T3 line "לא נמצאו סימנים חריגים בתשלומים שנבחרו" (no ₪0 tile).
   - Phone 390: the panel collapses to one line with a count, and the owner's release works on the phone.
6. **Remittance note (A19b).** `payRun.remittanceText({supplierId, invoiceIds, paidOn})` (finance) returns `{he, en}` plain text for the supplier, plus `wa.me` and `mailto` links built from the supplier's contacts (W2-11 normaliser), and copy-to-clipboard. Content:
   - the invoices paid with their totals;
   - offsets (`payment_deductions`, G-03) with claim numbers: "קוזז: ₪100.00 · דרישת זיכוי 0042 · חוסר 2 ק״ג סלמון";
   - invoices **held on an undecided gap**: "חשבונית MF-25010 בבדיקה · נעדכן" with **no ₪ and no reason** (decision 18);
   - the net.
   Nothing is sent by the server; zero outbox rows target a supplier (test). W2-03's print view stays the printable version.
7. **Roles and tenancy.** All procedures are restaurant-scoped; a foreign `supplierId`, `changeId` or `invoiceId` → NOT_FOUND with B untouched. The bank account shows `account_last4` only, never the full number (test on the payload).
8. **No ₪ moved.** Pre-flight is read-only; the hero, pay-run totals and letters are identical before/after a pre-flight read (test). Flags never become a `DiscrepancyType`, a claim or leak ₪.
9. **Off switch and KPI hook.** Option `payRunPreflight` off → no panel, no guard on soft flags; the **hard bank hold stays on regardless** (AD12), and the PR says so. KPI: audit `payrun.bank_change_confirmed`, `payrun.preflight_blocked` (written when `markPaid` refuses), the acknowledged kinds in the paid audit (A19). AU-07 reads "duplicates held before payment (count, ₪)".

Where the docs conflict: the spec's "dispute-aware remittance naming withheld claims" would name undecided gaps to the supplier. Decision 18 wins: only decided claims and offsets are named; undecided holds read "בבדיקה". W2-03 AC 9's print view is reused, not rebuilt.
