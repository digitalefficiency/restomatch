# AU-18 · Month-end missing-documents report, and later the Tax Authority cross-check
**One line:** on the 8th of every month, a week before the VAT report on the 15th, the bookkeeper gets one list per supplier of what is missing for last month: delivery notes nobody billed, invoices with no delivery note, invoices above ₪5,000 without an allocation number, and credits the supplier still owes. Later, with a Tax Authority credential, the list also shows invoices the supplier **reported** to the Tax Authority that never reached the restaurant.
- **Impact (assumption):** a manual "דוח חוסרים" of 1–3 h/month falls to ~15 min: bookkeeper 0.17–0.63 h/week ≈ **₪65–245/month time-₪** (₪89/h; labour value: an external bookkeeper paid a flat fee saves the restaurant cash only if the fee falls). Money (ITA part only, later): input VAT on supplier invoices never captured, 0–1 a month × ₪2,500 × 18% ≈ **₪0–450/month**, lumpy, ₪0 for 0%-VAT produce. Missing documents are usually timing, not leak: nothing here touches the leak figure. Never summed.
- **Today:** the parts exist, scattered and pull-only: delivery-note coverage per supplier (`packages/api/src/ledger/coverage.ts:77-188`, flag `supplierLedger`), VAT at risk (`packages/api/src/vat/vatAtRisk.ts:96-178`, flag `vatAtRisk`), open claims (`recovery/service.ts`), and an empty month-close router (`packages/api/src/routers/close.ts:10`). No report joins them, nothing runs on the 8th, nothing reaches the bookkeeper by itself.
**Owner agent:** ocr-procurement-engineer (owns the worker job; web-feature-engineer for the close-desk panel; leak-economist signs that no figure enters the leak and that VAT is shown apart; tenant-security-warden reviews the ITA adapter boundary). **Device:** desktop (bookkeeper) + e-mail. **Effort:** M (report: 2 PRs, a = builder + procedure + panel, b = 8th-of-month job) + L (ITA diff, PR c, only after a human obtains the credential). **humanPR:** no (the 8th slot is H-05a row 2; the ITA credential is Phase-6, human-only, AD14).

## 5. Acceptance criteria
