# AU-20 · Coexistence by documents: the restaurant forwards its MarketMan or Zestt orders, and we match against them without retyping
**One line:** a restaurant that orders in MarketMan or Zestt forwards each order (PDF or e-mail) to its RestoMatch intake address. The order becomes a PO with its supplier and per-line SKU, labelled "יובא מ-MarketMan" / "יובא מ-Zestt", and the engine matches the invoice against it line by line. Nobody retypes the order, nothing is written back to the platform, and the receiver sees the expected lines with no ₪.
- PR a: the existing Zestt import becomes platform-aware and gets a MarketMan PO layout; an intake document can be turned into a PO with one manager action; a **new** supplier name needs a manager confirm before the PO can be matched.
- PR b: senders the owner marks as "order source" are routed to the PO import automatically.
- PR c (later, gated): Zestt digital delivery notes become **expected quantities** for the dock, never pre-filled counts.

Today:
- the PO import exists but is Zestt-only and manual: `importPo` takes `platform: z.literal('zestt')` and a base64 upload (`routers/orders.ts:694-735`); `importPurchaseOrder` hard-codes `'zestt'` in the lookup, the insert and the supplier backfill (`orders/importPo.ts:177, 241, 295, 308`);
- an unknown supplier name is **silently created** and adopted (`importPo.ts:273-315`), so a misread name makes a new supplier and a PO that matches nothing, or the wrong one;
- every document that reaches the intake address becomes an **invoice** (`ingest/pipeline.ts:117-125`, `source: 'email'`); a forwarded order is OCR'd as an invoice and lands in review as noise;
- without a PO only DUPLICATE, VAT, TOTAL and contract checks fire, so a MarketMan/Zestt restaurant sees a fraction of its leak (int-08 §2).

Impact (note 21 §2.2, ASSUMPTIONS for the ₪200k/month restaurant): manager 0.4–0.9 h/week of retyping avoided (14 orders × 2–4 min, minus ~0.25 min to forward) ≈ **₪180–400/month hours-₪**. Leak ₪ **surfaced, not new**: PO-dependent types are 40–70% of the leak and the tenant forwards 50–80% of POs → ₪3,000 × 0.4–0.7 × 0.5–0.8 = **₪600–1,680/month of leak made visible**. That is the existing engine's figure on a larger PO base, an upper bound (contract checks overlap), never recovered money. The two are never added.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Platform-aware import.** `importPurchaseOrder(db, { restaurantId, normalized, platform: 'zestt' | 'marketman', createdBy })`. Every `'zestt'` literal in `importPo.ts` reads `platform`. Re-importing the same `(restaurant, platform, externalId)` updates in place; a Zestt and a MarketMan order with the same external id are two POs (test). The refusal once a receipt exists (186-199) is kept byte-for-byte.
2. **MarketMan layout (PR a, needs the 3 samples).** `marketmanDocument.ts` parses a MarketMan PO PDF into `NormalizedPurchaseOrder` with supplier name, order number, delivery date, lines (SKU when printed, description, qty, unit, unit price) and printed totals. The checksum rule is unchanged: a fail → `draft`. Golden fixtures: the 3 samples parse to the expected lines exactly.
3. **"זו הזמנה" from the intake inbox.** `coexist.importForwardedPo({ documentId, platform })` (manager) reads the stored bytes of an intake document in `needs_review`, imports it as a PO, sets the document to `status 'skipped'`, `error 'po_imported'`, writes audit `coexist.po_imported` (`after: { poId, platform, checksumOk }`), and never creates an invoice. A document already registered as an invoice is refused: "המסמך כבר נקלט כחשבונית".
4. **Supplier confirm.** When `resolveSupplier` finds no exact name match, the supplier is created as today **but** the PO is forced to `draft` and listed in `coexist.pendingSupplierLinks`. The manager sees: "ספק חדש מהזמנה שהועברה: ׳ירקות השרון׳ · [זה ספק חדש] [זה ספק קיים ▾]". "ספק קיים" moves the PO to the chosen supplier and deactivates the new row (`setActive`); either choice moves the PO to `sent` only if its checksum passed. Audit `coexist.supplier_confirmed`. A PO in `draft` is never matched (`buildMatchInput.ts:40`, test).
5. **Label.** Every imported PO shows the chip "יובא מ-MarketMan" / "יובא מ-Zestt" (T4 neutral) on the orders list, the PO page and the invoice's match panel. English: "Imported from MarketMan".
6. **Auto-route by sender (PR b).** The owner marks a sender address as an order source: "כתובת זו שולחת הזמנות מ-MarketMan" (`coexist.setPoSender`, owner). With `coexistDocs` on, a document from that sender goes to `forwardedPo` instead of becoming an invoice (one hook in `processInboundMessage`, owned by AU-10). A document whose parse fails stays in `needs_review` with "לא הצלחנו לקרוא את ההזמנה — בדקו ידנית"; it is never silently dropped.
7. **Receiver: no ₪.** The receiver sees the expected lines of an imported PO (name, qty, unit) and never `total_estimated`, `unit_price_expected` or the checksum figures (test through the receiver role on every procedure that returns an imported PO).
8. **No leak change on the same data.** With the lab fixtures, the hero before and after importing a PO for an invoice that had none changes only by the PO-dependent findings the engine already defines; the leak-canary is unmoved; no MISSING_ON_INVOICE ₪ enters any figure.
9. **Copy (he/en).** Forwarding instructions on the intake settings card (growth-pmm-engineer): "מזמינים ב-MarketMan או ב-Zestt? העבירו כל הזמנה לכתובת הקליטה שלכם, ונשווה אותה לחשבונית בלי להקליד מחדש." No "דליפה חדשה"; the surfaced figure is called "נבדק מול הזמנה".
10. **KPI hook.** Audit `coexist.po_imported`, `coexist.supplier_confirmed`, `coexist.po_parse_failed`. AU-07 reports per week: POs imported by platform, share of invoices matched against an imported PO, minutes saved (ASSUMPTION 2–4 min each, labelled).
