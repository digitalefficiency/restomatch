## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **SKU on synced lines (C1).** `syncOrders` writes `supplier_sku` from the platform product id and runs `decideLink` per line: auto-link only on SKU or alias; anything else is a review item, never a silent product.
2. **Never eligible without a key.** A synced PO with any line that has neither SKU nor product stays out of matching until mapped (test against `buildMatchInput`). Copy on the PO: "יש שורות בלי קישור למוצר — ההזמנה לא נבדקת מול חשבונית עד שיקושרו".
3. **Upsert, never delete (C2).** Lines are upserted by `lineKey(platformLineId ?? sku ?? normalised description + unit)`. A line that a `gr_lines.po_line_id` points at is never deleted or re-keyed; a platform line that disappears is marked cancelled in `notes`, not removed. Interim key until H-04: the same composite AU-20 uses; with H-04, `external_line_id`.
4. **Our status wins after the dock (C3).** `statusPrecedence(ours, theirs)`: once a goods receipt exists, the platform may move nothing; platform `closed`/`cancelled` is stored as metadata (`notes` tag `platform_status:<s>`) and shown "סגור ב-MarketMan". Before any receipt, platform `cancelled` cancels. Table-driven test over every pair.
5. **Unmapped supplier is visible.** A skipped order increments a per-connection counter read by `health.ts`; no `console.warn`-only path remains.
6. **Price list = expected price only (PR b, C4).** `listProducts` produces a diff in W3-10's shape. The owner confirms in "מה ישתנה"; only then is `supplier_catalog_items.list_price` written. A list price never becomes a contract unless the owner locks it in W2-12's screen. Where no contract exists, a PRICE_HIGHER found against a list price shows the basis label "מחירון ≠ חוזה" (leak-economist signs). No `price_history` row is written (count test).
7. **Health line (PR c, C6).** In W3-07a's hub: "MarketMan · סונכרן לפני 12 דק׳ · 3 הזמנות דולגו: ספק לא מקושר · [לקשר ספקים]". One fix CTA. `sync_cursor` written per run (C8). The line is owner/manager only.
8. **Schedule (PR c, human).** The PR body carries the H-05a row-5 proposal: `sync-platforms`, every 30 min, `tz: Asia/Jerusalem`, skippable by `SERVERLESS_CRON_SKIP`, tenant loop isolates failures. A human authors the `cron.ts` change.
9. **Imported receipts (PR d, C5).** Platform receipts become `goods_receipts` with `source = 'platform'` (H-04), labelled "נקלט ב-MarketMan" (T4). A receipt made in our app for the same PO is never overwritten; the platform receipt is then stored as a second, read-only record and flagged "שתי קבלות לאותה הזמנה — בדקו". Receiver views: quantities only, no ₪.
10. **No write-back, no ₪ to the receiver, no engine change.** Grep test: no adapter write call in the sync path. Receiver role on every PO/receipt read: no ₪ field. Canary green; hero unchanged on lab data until a real sync runs.
11. **KPI hook.** Audit `coexist.sync_run` (`after: { platform, orders, skippedUnlinked, linesNeedingLink }`), `coexist.pricelist_diff_confirmed`, `coexist.receipt_imported`. AU-07 reports per week: share of invoices matched against a synced PO, skipped orders, duplicate-receiving cases avoided.
