# AU-22 · Supplier negotiation pack: the owner walks into a supplier meeting with one page of facts, and nothing is ever sent by the system
**One line:** one owner-only page and printable PDF per supplier, for the last 3, 6 or 12 months: what we spent, how each item's paid price moved, which rises cost ₪ per month, where the supplier billed above the agreed price, and our record with them (discrepancies found, claims sent, credited, days to credit, fill rate as a ₪-free %). The owner picks the sections, then prints or shares it **himself**. A reply draft to a price-rise notice is prepared on request.
- Three ways in: the owner opens a supplier; AU-13's "contract ends in ≤14 days" line; a price-list diff with a rise ≥3% (AU-13 section 5 / W3-10).
- Two ₪ kinds sit in separate columns and are never summed: **"מחירים שעלו"** (avoided-cost ₪/month, A18 method) and **"דליפה"** (leak ₪ from the engine, A12 record).

Today:
- the facts exist but on five screens: the supplier leak card (`apps/web/app/dashboard/suppliers/[id]/leak-card-data.ts:40-72`, flag `supplierLeakCard`, `features.ts:44`), the ledger tab (`supplierLedger`), the contract tab (`contracts`), the recovery desk (W2-10) and price alerts (off in the pilot);
- the only printable supplier artefact is the leak card print page (`suppliers/[id]/leak-card/page.tsx:24-95`), which shows leak only and no price history;
- the supplier scorecard (`packages/charts/src/suppliers.ts:19-29`: clean-match %, average price delta %, duplicates, trend) is plan-gated (`advanced_analytics`) and per restaurant, not per supplier history;
- nothing prepares the owner's answer to a supplier's price-rise notice.

Impact (note 21 §2.2, ASSUMPTIONS): preparing for a supplier meeting today means opening ledger, contracts, recovery and alerts: 45–90 min per meeting, 1–2 meetings/month. The pack saves ≈0.1–0.5 h/week × 4.33 × ₪103 = **₪45–225/month owner hours-₪** (₪65–325 at ₪150). **No additive money ₪:** the pack is the "act" step of A18's avoided cost (₪100–600/month, AU-13) and makes that range more reachable; AU-07 measures alerts → negotiations → price change. The claim figures shown are A12's leak ₪, in their own column.

**Owner agent:** **web-feature-engineer** (`apps/web`, `packages/api` routers). **matching-engine-guardian** owns the one new charts file (`packages/charts/src/supplierPack.ts`, read-only SQL over `price_history`, `invoice_lines`, `recovery_claims`, `gr_lines`); **money-format-marshal** signs every ₪ string (screen and PDF); **leak-economist** signs the two-column rule and the creep wording; **tenant-security-warden** reviews the owner-only procedure; **rtl-design-steward** signs the LIGHT print layout. **Device:** desktop (print/PDF); readable on phone. **Effort:** M. ## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Owner only.** `supplierPack.get/pdf/replyDraft` are `ownerProcedure`. Manager, bookkeeper, chef and receiver get FORBIDDEN (test per role); the page 404s for them through the dashboard guard.
2. **Period.** 3 / 6 / 12 months, Israel calendar months, default 6. Header: "שטראוס · 1.5.2026–31.10.2026 · הוכן ב-7.11.2026".
3. **Spend.** Invoiced ₪ before VAT for the supplier in the period, per month (sparkline) and total, from the same source as the leak card. Label "רכש מהספק".
4. **Price history (paid price).** Top 15 items by spend: first and last paid unit price in the period, % change, a 12-point sparkline from `price_history`. Line: "חזה עוף · ₪31.90 ← ₪35.40 לק״ג · +11%". `old ← new` order per W2-13's `PriceMove`.
5. **"מחירים שעלו" (avoided-cost ₪/month).** Items with a sustained rise, each with W2-12's "≈₪212 בחודש (הערכה)"; a column total labelled "≈₪640 בחודש (הערכה) — לא חלק מהדליפה". Unknown → "אין מספיק נתונים", ranked last.
6. **Agreed vs billed.** With `contracts` on: each contract item billed above its price in force, count and ₪ over contract (the engine's existing finding ₪, from the leak breakdown), plus "חוזה מסתיים ב-30.11" when `valid_to` ≤ 60 days.
7. **Our record ("דליפה").** Discrepancies found (count, ₪ leak excl. MISSING_ON_INVOICE, matching `/dashboard/leaks` for the same filter: parity test), claims sent (count, ₪), credited (count, ₪), median days to credit, claims overdue (>7 days, `OVERDUE_DAYS`). Its own column; never added to the creep column (test: no rendered number equals their sum unless by coincidence of the fixtures, and the two totals carry different labels).
8. **Fill rate (₪-free).** % of ordered lines received in full on the first delivery, from PO vs GR lines; shown as "92% מההזמנות הגיעו מלאות". No ₪.
9. **Opt-in sections and redaction.** A section picker; default on = sections 4, 6, 7; spend, creep and fill rate are opt-in for the shared copy. The shared/PDF view never contains internal notes, decision comments, staff names, approval routing or the restaurant's other suppliers (test on the PDF text).
10. **PDF.** LIGHT, RTL, A4, the restaurant's name and address (H-03 when present), page numbers, "הוכן על ידי RestoMatch עבור <מסעדה>". Every ₪ through `moneyText`; no inline `Intl.NumberFormat` (grep test).
11. **Reply draft (PR b).** From a price-rise line or an AU-13 link: "שלום <ספק>, קיבלנו את עדכון המחירים מ-12.11. לפי המחיר המוסכם בינינו, חזה עוף ₪31.90 לק״ג עד 31.12. נשמח להסבר לשינוי." Shown for copy, or opened in the owner's own mail/WhatsApp client. **No server send, no outbox row** (test: zero `notification_outbox` rows after `replyDraft`).
12. **Triggers (PR b).** AU-13's contract-expiry and price-list lines carry "[הכינו חבילת מו״מ]" to `/dashboard/suppliers/<id>/pack?from=a18` (owner recipients only; the manager's copy of the mail omits the link).
13. **KPI hook.** Audit `supplier.pack_opened`, `supplier.pack_exported` (`after: { sections, period }`), `supplier.reply_drafted`. AU-07 reports per month: packs exported per supplier, and for those suppliers the count of items whose paid price fell or held in the next 60 days (alerts → negotiation → price change).
