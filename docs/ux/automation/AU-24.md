# AU-24 · Agreed price on every order the chef or manager sends, opt-in per supplier
**One line:** when the owner opts a supplier in, every order sent to that supplier (e-mail, PDF or "מהוואטסאפ שלי") prints, per line, the **contract price in force** on the order date: "חזה עוף · 20 ק״ג · מחיר מוסכם ₪31.90 לק״ג". Lines with no contract price show no price. The supplier sees the agreed price before invoicing, and a later PRICE_HIGHER claim can say "the order you received said ₪31.90". The sender still taps send; nothing goes out by itself.

Today (most of the machinery exists):
- flag `orderPricesOut` (`lib/features.ts:37`) switches the outbound order document on (`routers/orders.ts:940-975`): SKU, **every PO unit price**, line totals and a PDF on e-mail;
- the price-showing switch is **restaurant-wide** and defaults to **show** (`orders/document/load.ts:21-25`, `showPrices: s?.showPrices !== false`; set by `setDocumentSettings`, `orders.ts:1084-1101`);
- "(מחיר חוזה)" is printed only where the PO price equals the contract price in force to the agora (`load.ts:15-19, 89-97, 110`; `contracts/orderPrices.ts:16` `contractPricesForOrder`; `orders/document/text.ts:20-28`). Any other PO price (last price, list price) is printed too, unlabelled;
- so with the flag on, a supplier can receive a non-agreed price that looks like a commitment, and there is no per-supplier choice.

Impact (note 21 §2.2, ASSUMPTIONS): manager ≈0–0.1 h/week of disputes avoided = **₪0–45/month hours-₪**. **No additive money ₪:** it works inside the A12 recovery pool (₪270–720/month recovered, catalogue §F row 1) in two ways, fewer PRICE_HIGHER at source and stronger claim evidence. AU-07 measures PRICE_HIGHER per opted-in supplier before and after. Never added to the hours.

**Owner agent:** **web-feature-engineer** (`packages/api` orders document, `apps/web`). **money-format-marshal** signs the printed ₪ (text, WhatsApp, PDF); **leak-economist** signs "agreed = contract in force only"; **tenant-security-warden** reviews the owner-only setting and the receiver redaction; **rtl-design-steward** the PDF line. **Device:** both (owner sets on desktop; chef/manager send from the phone). **Effort:** S. **humanPR:** no. The per-supplier list lives in `restaurants.settings.orderDocument.agreedPriceSupplierIds` (jsonb, the same object `setDocumentSettings` writes); no schema, no engine, no `cron.ts`.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Per-supplier opt-in, owner only.** `agreedPrice.setSupplier({ supplierId, on })` is `ownerProcedure`; it adds/removes the id in `settings.orderDocument.agreedPriceSupplierIds` with a jsonb merge (never overwriting `showPrices`), audit `orders.agreed_price_set`. Manager/chef/bookkeeper/receiver FORBIDDEN (test). Default: empty list.
2. **What prints.** For an opted-in supplier, with `orderPricesOut` on: a line shows "מחיר מוסכם ₪31.90 לק״ג" **only** when `contractPricesForOrder` returns a price in force on the order date for (product, unit) **and** the PO price equals it to the agora; otherwise the line shows no price and no "(ללא מחיר)" note. No last-price or list-price ever prints for such a supplier. The order total prints only when every line is agreed.
3. **Not opted in.** Behaviour is exactly today's (snapshot of the existing document tests unchanged).
4. **Mismatch guard.** If a PO line's price differs from the contract in force, the send preview shows the manager/chef "המחיר בהזמנה (₪33.50) שונה מהמחיר המוסכם (₪31.90) — יישלח בלי מחיר" before the tap; the printed line carries no price.
5. **Every path prints the same.** E-mail text, PDF and the W2-06 "מהוואטסאפ שלי" text all come from `loadOrderDocument`; a test renders the three and compares the price tokens.
6. **Receiver and roles.** The receiver never sees the agreed price: not on the PO screens, not in the WhatsApp/PDF text he opens (W2-06 `redactPrices`; test). The chef sees it on orders (allowed). No notification carries it.
7. **₪ format.** Through the document's agorot formatter → `moneyText` once G-13 merges; no inline `Intl.NumberFormat` (grep test). `he-IL` numerals; the ₪ token is bidi-isolated (`orders/document/bidi.ts`).
8. **Settings copy.** "מחיר מוסכם בהזמנות לספק: הספק יראה בכל הזמנה את המחיר שסיכמתם, רק לפי חוזה בתוקף. [שטראוס ✓] [תנובה ✗]". English equivalent.
9. **KPI hook.** Audit `orders.agreed_price_set`; per send, the existing order audit gains `after.agreedLines` (count). AU-07 reports per opted-in supplier: PRICE_HIGHER count and ₪ in the 60 days before vs after opt-in, labelled "השוואה, לא סיבתיות".
