# AU-25 · Food-cost % lite (OWNER DECISION OD-C7): one sales number a month turns the leak into food-cost points
**One line:** once a month the owner or bookkeeper types one number, food sales before VAT. The monthly report then shows one line: a **purchases-based** food-cost % and the month's leak in food-cost points: "עלות מזון לפי רכש: 31.2% · הדליפה החודש = 0.5 נקודות עלות מזון".
- It always carries the label "לפי רכש, לא לפי ספירה" and a neutral reference band 28–35% (Israeli consultants [3P/V], ext-09 §2.10).
- It is never a hero, never a dashboard tile, never a second leak. There is no money action.
- It is an **OWNER DECISION** (OD-C7): it comes near the LOSE·D "P&L / food cost" rows (note 20 M#12–13) without POS, stock counts or inventory. Build behind an option that is **off** until the owner answers yes.

  - `packages/api/src/reports/monthly.ts` `MonthlyReportData` 44-71 (`leakAgorot` 49, `spendAgorot` 50, `leakPct` 51 = leak ÷ spend, `returnedAgorot` 55);
  - `computeMonthlyReport` 144, `ensureMonthlyReport` 233 (snapshot), `monthlyReportEmail` 418;
- the only sales data path is a POS **file** import per item and day (`schema.ts` `pos_sales_imports` 1446-1467, `pos_sales` 1469-1494; `recipes/pos.ts:111` `posVsTheory`), behind `recipes` and `integrations` (flags OFF in the pilot);

- so an owner who wants "what % of sales is food" keeps his own spreadsheet, and the leak never speaks his language.

- The two are never added.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Formula (pure, integer).** `foodCostBp = round_half_up((spendAgorot − creditsAgorot) × 10,000 ÷ salesAgorot)`; `leakPointsBp = round_half_up(leakAgorot × 10,000 ÷ salesAgorot)`. `spendAgorot` and `leakAgorot` are the report's own fields for the month (same numbers as the report's headline: parity test); `creditsAgorot` = credits recovered in the month (`returnedAgorot`). One rounding, at the end. Display to one decimal: "31.2%", "0.5 נקודות".
2. **Input.** `setSalesFigure({ month: 'YYYY-MM', salesAgorot })` (owner, bookkeeper). Input via `parseDecimalInput` (G-13) as ₪ before VAT; audit `report.sales_figure_set` (`before`, `after`). Months in the future are refused; a closed month (H-07) can be corrected and the line shows "תוקן ב-12.12".
3. **Plausibility.** If the result is <10% or >70%, the input is not refused but the line shows "המספר נראה חריג — בדקו את סכום המכירות" and no reference band.
4. **The report line.** Summary tab only, under the leak headline: "עלות מזון לפי רכש: 31.2% (טווח מקובל בענף: 28–35%) · הדליפה החודש = 0.5 נקודות עלות מזון · לפי רכש, לא לפי ספירה". English: "Purchases-based food cost: 31.2% … not count-based". No sales figure → the line is absent (never "0%"), with "הוסיפו מכירות החודש כדי לראות עלות מזון" for owner/bookkeeper only.
5. **Not a hero.** The value appears nowhere but the report line and the e-mail/PDF tabs of the same report (test: the component is imported only under `reports/**`; no `dash/home/**` or hero import).
6. **Roles.** Owner reads and types; bookkeeper types and reads; manager reads the report as today but the line is hidden unless the owner opts managers in (default hidden); **chef and receiver** never see it (test on the procedure and the rendered report payload).
7. **Leak unchanged.** `leakAgorot` in the report and the hero are byte-identical with the option on and off (test); MISSING_ON_INVOICE never enters `leakAgorot` (after #211).
8. **Off switch.** Option `foodCostLite` default **off** (AU-01 registry, owner-toggleable only after OD-C7). Off → no line, no checklist step, no input surface.
9. **KPI hook.** Audit `report.sales_figure_set`; AU-07 reports per month: tenants with the line on, months with a sales figure, and the share of owners opening the report (existing report-open telemetry) before vs after.
