# AU-26 · Pack-size hint within one supplier (behind the icp-keeper gate): "the 5 kg is 9% cheaper per kg than the 2.5 kg, from the same supplier"
**One line:** on an order draft (AU-11 / W3-09) and on the owner's price-rise mail (AU-13), when the **same supplier's own price list** sells the same product in two pack sizes and the larger is cheaper per base unit, the chef or owner sees one hint: "אותו ספק: 5 ק״ג זול ב-9% לק״ג מ-2.5 ק״ג · ≈₪60 בחודש (הערכה) · רק אם נגמר בזמן". ## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Comparator (pure).** `comparePacks(rows)` takes active catalog rows of **one** supplier for **one** product. Per row: base-unit price = `list_price ÷ (pack_size × factor(unit → base))` in exact rational arithmetic, rounded half-up once to 1/100 agora. It returns the cheapest-per-base-unit row vs the row currently ordered, saving %, and nothing when the difference is <3%.
2. **Monthly estimate.** `≈₪N בחודש (הערכה)` = (price per base unit of current − cheaper) × the 90-day base-unit quantity bought ÷ 3 (W2-12's quantity basis), integer agorot, one half-up rounding. Hint shown only when ≥₪50/month.
3. **On the order draft (PR a).** Under the line: "אותו ספק: 5 ק״ג זול ב-9% לק״ג מ-2.5 ק״ג · ≈₪60 בחודש (הערכה) · רק אם נגמר בזמן". Actions: "החליפו לאריזה הזו בהזמנה הזו" (changes **this** draft line only, after a tap) or "לא רלוונטי" (hides for this product for 90 days, audit). No default change, no saved preference that alters future drafts.
4. **Owner mail section (PR b).** In AU-13's 07:00 digest, owner and manager only: "אריזות משתלמות יותר אצל אותו ספק: 2 מוצרים · ≈₪110 בחודש (הערכה)", top 3. The **chef's** mail never carries it (AU-13 builds chef payloads separately). The section is avoided cost: never the word "דליפה".
5. **Roles.** `packHint.forDraft` is `chefProcedure` (chef/manager/owner). Receiver and bookkeeper FORBIDDEN. The hint never appears on receiving screens.
6. **Scope test.** Static test over `packHint/**`: no `product_group_id`, no join or `IN` over more than one `supplier_id`, no write to `po_lines`/`supplier_catalog_items`/`price_history`.
7. **No leak change.** Hero, claims and the canary unchanged with the option on (test).
8. **Off switch.** Option `packHint` default **off** (AU-01 registry, owner-toggleable). Off → no hint, no section.
9. **KPI hook.** Audit `orders.pack_hint_shown`, `orders.pack_hint_applied`, `orders.pack_hint_dismissed`. AU-07 reports per month: hints shown, applied %, and avoided-cost ₪ estimated on applied hints, labelled "(הערכה) — לא חלק מהדליפה".
