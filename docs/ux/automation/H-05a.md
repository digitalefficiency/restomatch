**B. Why these times.**
- Sunday 08:00 = start of the Israeli work week, after the 06:00 receiver brief and the 07:00 price alerts, before the 08:30 recovery run (a bookkeeper sees one digest, then the recovery list).
- 8th 08:00 = a week before the 15th VAT report, after late invoices of the previous month arrive. If the 8th is a Saturday, G-06 moves delivery to after Shabbat (the job still runs and computes; `notify()` sets `scheduled_at`).
- 08:15 daily = after price alerts, before recovery; it never competes with 08:30 for the per-person cap of 6.
- Every 15 min 06:00–21:45 (README §8 now says the same) = the body sends "טיוטה מוכנה" once per supplier per day (AD9: 2 h lead). The rule, identical in AU-11: every job is enqueued with `data: {}` (`cron.ts:56-142`) and `WorkerJob` carries no slot time (`queue.ts:75-82`), so the body knows only `now`. It computes its **bucket** = `now` floored to :00/:15/:30/:45 in Asia/Jerusalem, and pushes for suppliers whose cut-off is in the half-open window **[bucket + 2 h, bucket + 2 h 15 min)**. Every cut-off falls in exactly one bucket; the per-supplier-per-day dedupe stops doubles; a run that executes late still uses its own bucket, so it never pushes after the cut-off (it may miss one push after an outage; acceptable). IL wall time outside 06:00–21:59 → skip. Nothing between 22:00 and 06:00.
- **Cut-offs before 08:00** can never get a 2 h push (the first bucket, 06:00, serves cut-offs 08:00–08:14). They get no intraday push: the previous day's 09:00 run already prepares the draft and its message names the cut-off ("טיוטה מוכנה ל<ספק> · השליחה עד 07:30 מחר"), AU-11 AC; say so in the PR body.

**C. Header correction (no behaviour change).** Replace the `sync-platforms` bullet (`cron.ts:9-10`) with: "sync-platforms — NOT scheduled. The consumer exists (handlers.ts); a schedule is added only when a tenant has live MarketMan/Zester credentials (Phase-6, human)". Register nothing for it now.
