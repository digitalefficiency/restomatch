# IA-01 + IA-03 · density budgets as a check, visible role lenses

**One line:** IA-01 measures every home, queue and record against the rule-4 density budget (words, numbers, T2 marks, primary buttons, blocks; "one figure once") in **report mode**; IA-03 makes **one role-lens table** the source for what each role sees, gives the 403 page a role-specific next step, and opens the 10 blocked screens **read-only** to the bookkeeper (owner decision I3).

## 4. Slices
**IA-01 · density check (report mode)**
1. `e2e/ia/budget.ts`: the rule-4 table per screen type `home · queue · record · audit · field · setup` × desk/phone: blocks in the fold, numbers in the fold, T2 marks (screen and per row/card), visible explanation words, primary buttons; plus the "figure once" rule (a `data-figure` id twice outside an `audit` screen, or two values for one id across screens).
2. `evaluateDensity(type, vp, counts) → Finding[]` and `checkFigures(…)`: pure, unit-tested in vitest.
3. `e2e/ia/meter.ts`: one `page.evaluate` that counts **inside the first fold**: words (visible text), numbers (`data-num`/`<bdi>` money, else digit tokens), T2 marks (computed colour, background or border = `--c-danger*` / `--c-warn*`, nested marks collapsed), primary buttons (`data-primary`, else filled `--c-primary` buttons/links), blocks (`data-block`, else the direct card children of `<main>` that intersect the fold).
4. `e2e/ia/screens.ts`: homes × 5 roles, queues (approvals, recovery, pay run, inbox, receiving board), records (invoice, order, supplier; ids resolved from the seed), desk 1440×900 and phone 390×844.
5. `IA-01.spec.ts`: logs every finding, attaches `ia-01-report.json`, **never fails** (report mode). `IA01_ENFORCE=1` turns findings over the ratchet allowance into failures (wave 4, H-09ʰ).

**IA-03 · role lenses**
a. `roleLens/lens.ts`: per role `{ money: 'all'|'orders+recipes'|'none', quantities, reads[], edits[] }` + `routeAccess(role, path) → 'edit'|'read'|'none'` (from `ROUTE_ROLES` + `READ_ONLY_ROUTES`).
b. `nextStepFor(role, path)`: the role's own next step for a 403 (receiver → "לקבלת סחורה", chef → "להזמנות", …) and `canEverOpen(role, path)`: no "בקשו גישה" for a screen that role never gets (₪ screens for R/C).
c. `READ_ONLY_ROUTES`: the 10 screens (orders, receiving, suppliers, catalog, products, recipes, inventory, team, reports/monthly, reports/digest) read-only for the bookkeeper (I3), consumed by the registry through one orchestrator line.
d. `linkableFor(role, href)`: "no link to a screen the viewer cannot open" (e.g. breakdown → report for B), for the shell/drill links.
e. A generated table of the remaining `role === …` checks (file, line, the lens call that replaces it), handed to the owning packages.
