# G-07 · Capability model: who sees, separate from who decides
**One line:** one server-side capability table (`CAP`) plus `can(cap)`, split into who may read an item (`VISIBLE_TO`) and who may decide it (`ACT_ON`). Today the bookkeeper reads "all handled" while 4 items (₪296) wait on the owner, the chef cannot order, and receiver/chef activity feeds leak ₪. This makes those role rules one table, tested per procedure, instead of four drifting copies. **Owner agent:** web-feature-engineer (tenant-security-warden **mandatory** reviewer). **Device:** both (server-only, no UI). **Effort:** M. **humanPR:** no.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. `lib/roles.ts` exports explicit tables:
   - `VISIBLE_TO: Record<UserRole, ReadonlyArray<UserRole|'unrouted'>>`, where owner, manager and bookkeeper see all routings and receiver/chef see `[]`;
   - `ACT_ON` = today's semantics exactly: owner → owner, manager, bookkeeper, unrouted; manager → manager, unrouted; bookkeeper → bookkeeper; receiver/chef → none.
   `canActOn(role, requiredRole)` keeps its signature and reads `ACT_ON`. `expandVisibleRoles` keeps returning today's values (deprecated alias), so `myQueue` behaviour is unchanged until W2-04. An exhaustive 5 × 6 role × requiredRole table test pins both.
2. `lib/capabilities.ts` exports:
   - `CAP` (the table in §4), `type Cap`;
   - `hasCap(role, cap)`;
   - `canOn(ctx, cap, { createdBy })`, the own-row rule for `order.draft`/`order.send` below manager;
   - `redactPrices(ctx, row, fields)`, which nulls the price fields when `!price.view`.
   `trpc.ts` exports `can(cap)`, a builder that adds `meta: { cap }` and a FORBIDDEN middleware with `appCode` `capability.required`.
3. Every existing tier procedure carries `meta.tier` (`public|authed|userScoped|member|owner|manager|receiver|bookkeeper|chef|invoiceReader|finance|admin`). No runtime behaviour changes, proven by `rbac.test.ts` and the full `cross-tenant.attack.test.ts` unchanged and green.
4. `capabilities.test.ts` enumerates `appRouter._def.procedures` and fails on:
   - any procedure without `meta.tier` or `meta.cap`;
   - any **mutation** on bare `member` tier that is not in an explicit allow-list with a reason (e.g. `settings.setMyLocale`).
   It allow-lists the public procedures of the immutable `leads.ts`, `plans.*` and `growth.pricing`. It only adds to COVERAGE.
5. `activity.feed` for roles without `money.view` (receiver and chef by default):
   - `detail` is nulled for money-bearing kinds;
   - metadata keys matching `/(Ils|Agorot)$|amount|savings|total|price/i` are dropped;
   - no string in the response contains `₪`.
   Owner, manager and bookkeeper responses are deep-equal to before. `activity.feed` stays declared only in the base COVERAGE; no fragment re-declares it.
6. Matrix snapshot (`role-matrix.test.ts`, over `CAP` + `canOn`):
   - the chef drafts and edits their own draft, cannot touch a manager's draft, and **can send their own order**. The owner decision overrides GAP-PACKAGES G-07 §5 ("cannot place an order"); say so in the PR;
   - the bookkeeper may approve intake, correct invoice lines and edit account codes;
   - the receiver may not approve intake (`cross-tenant.attack.test.ts:2299` still passes), has no `ledger.view` and no `money.view`;
   - receiver/chef access to `owner.*` is unchanged (H-11).
7. Open question, not decided here: may the receiver draft orders? rootcause-7 suggests yes; no owner decision exists. Leave it out of `order.draft` and list it in the PR body.
