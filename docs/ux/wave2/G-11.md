## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **`registry.ts`** has zero imports (it must load inside `global-error`) and holds the single ordered `LEGAL_LINKS`: (1) `accessibility` → `/accessibility`, "הצהרת נגישות" / "Accessibility statement"; (2) `privacy` → `/privacy`, "מדיניות פרטיות"; (3) `terms` → `/terms`, "תנאי שימוש"; (4) `cookies` → `/cookies`, "מדיניות עוגיות"; (5) `cookieSettings` → `/cookies#cookie-settings`, "הגדרות עוגיות", `kind: 'action'` (H-02 intercepts it to open the sheet; until then it lands on the policy). Labels are `Localized {he, en}` (parity by type). It also exports `SUPPORT_LINKS` (help, contact) for W2-08 and `legalHref(id)`. `MARKETING_ROUTES`, the sitemap and the robots `allow` list derive from it; **robots now lists `/accessibility`**.
2. **Registry test:** every page entry (hash stripped, `kind !== 'action'`) passes `isPublicPath()`, is in `sitemap()` output, is in robots' `allow` list and matches no `disallow`. `publicPaths.ts` gains no entry (the warden checks the diff).
3. **Required-set test,** hard-coded and independent of the registry: for each of accessibility, privacy, terms, cookies — `app/(marketing)/<x>/page.tsx` exists, `isPublicPath('/<x>')` is true, and rendered `LegalLinks` markup contains the href.
4. **One component** `LegalLinks({locale, variant: 'footer-column'|'inline-row'|'plain'})`: server- and client-safe (no hooks, no `next/navigation`); `plain` uses `<a>` and inline styles for `global-error`; variants change layout only, never membership or order; it replaces the lists in MarketingShell, LegalShell, detail-sections, AuthShell and ShowcaseFooter. The inline privacy notices in LeadForm and LeakCheckForm (one link inside a sentence) use `legalHref('privacy')` instead of a literal (not a list, so the membership rule doesn't apply). Targets ≥44 px on phone (`touch-target`), tokens only, no `uppercase`/`tracking` on Hebrew, `data-legal-id` on every link.
5. **Footers where there were none:** `admin/layout.tsx`, `app/error.tsx` (`useLocale()` → `locale`), `app/global-error.tsx` (`plain`, locale from the cookie as today).
6. **`LegalShell`:** new `status?: 'draft' | 'approved'` (default `'draft'`); the banner and the per-clause tag (`LegalClause`) render only while `draft`; "עודכן לאחרונה" renders only with a date, never "—"; the related-documents row is `LegalLinks inline-row`. Visual output for `draft` is unchanged except the accessibility link added to the row.
7. **Statement wording** (he and en): cites ת״י 5568 at its legal level, WCAG 2.0 AA, with WCAG 2.2 AA as the target; no overlay widget.
8. **e2e, anonymous and per role:** these routes each show the links in the same order: `/`, `/pricing`, `/showcase`, the root 404; `/login`, `/login/check-email`, `/reset`, `/set-password`, `/invite/accept`, `/onboarding`; `/s/<valid|expired|unknown>`; `/admin`; `/dashboard` and `/dashboard/settings` (`test.fixme("awaiting W2-08 footer")` until it merges); `/scans/<id>` (`test.fixme("awaiting W2-02")`). Anonymous `/accessibility` answers 200 with no redirect, in he and en (WCAG 3.2.6).
