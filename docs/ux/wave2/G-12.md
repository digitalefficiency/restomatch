## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **`awaitingDecision(db, ctx, {scope, filters?})`.** `scope` is `'mine'|'all'`; `filters` are the approvals `QueueFilters` shape (types, severities, supplierIds, invoiceIds).
   - It reads **all** open/escalated rows of the tenant (`notZeroShortTwin()` applied; no limit or cursor) and returns: `count`, `blockers`, `oldestAt`, `ils {excl, incl, vat}` (never summed across bases; `mixedBasis: true` when >1 is non-zero), `reviewOnly {count}` (routed info / non-claimable rows, no ₪), `pendingLink {count, inclVatIls}`, and `byRole [{role, count, ils}]`.
   - The ₪ = `claimableLines` over the per-invoice leak (letters' rule, per-invoice cap).
   - Integer agorot inside, shekels at 2 dp out.
   - For a role without `money.view` (receiver, chef: owner decision), every `ils` field is `null`, and counts stay.
2. **Parity on the lab (25.10).** The owner's `scope:'mine'` equals today's `queueSummary`: 4 items, `toDecide` ₪296.00. The bookkeeper's `scope:'all'` returns `byRole` `[{role:'owner', count:4, ils:296.00}]`, so "ממתין לבעלים · 4 · ₪296.00", never "הכל טופל".
3. **Independence from paging.** On 120 seeded findings (info QTY_SHORT, UNORDERED_ARRIVAL, PRICE_LOWER, a DUPLICATE incl. VAT, a VAT_MISMATCH, an invoice where the cap binds) the result is independent of any limit or cursor. Its `ils.excl` = Σ `claimableLines` of the excl basis, and likewise for incl and vat. Info and PRICE_LOWER rows land in `reviewOnly` with no ₪.
4. **Ask periods.**
   - Every period tool output carries `{from, through, complete}` (restaurant time zone);
   - a new `leak_change` tool returns the aligned comparison with its span label ("1–25 באוק׳ מול 1–25 בספט׳") and both coverages;

5. **Grounding.** `groundAnswer` refuses (`grounded: false`, plus a neutral sentence) any answer whose delta compares a complete period with an incomplete one, or a figure without a span label.
6. **Ask's claimable and offline facts.** `claimableData` reads `awaitingDecision` (no `limit: 100`). The offline `leakChange` uses the same shared comparison function as the `leak_change` tool.
