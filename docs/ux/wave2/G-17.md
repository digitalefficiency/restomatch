# G-17 · Payment terms from the paper: printed terms and due date, and "terms shorter than agreed"
**One line:** OCR reads the payment terms and due date printed on the invoice. The pay run then compares them with what the restaurant agreed with the supplier. A supplier who quietly prints "שוטף+30" when the agreement is "שוטף+60", or prints a due date earlier than the agreement allows, is flagged before the money leaves.
- **Today:** terms are never read from the paper. `suppliers.payment_terms` is free text, and `invoices.due_date` is NULL in production. Nothing compares the paper with the agreement.
- **Owner decision (31.10):** payment terms go ALL in wave 2. That covers structured terms (W2-11), a computed due date (W2-03), OCR of the printed terms (this package) and the new "תנאים קצרים מהמוסכם" flag (this package + W2-03).

**Owner agent:** ocr-procurement-engineer (the OCR fields and their persistence). W2-03 renders the flag. leak-economist signs that the flag carries **no leak ₪**. **Device:** none (server only; W2-03 owns the UI). **Effort:** S–M. **humanPR:** no. It adds no column (the printed values live in `ocr_payload.raw`) and no `DiscrepancyType` (the 12-type union is immutable).

## 1. Start here
1. `packages/ocr/**`: the provider output type and the Claude Vision prompt / schema. Add two optional fields, `paymentTermsText` (the raw printed string, e.g. "תנאי תשלום: שוטף + 60") and `dueDate` (ISO date when printed, e.g. "לתשלום עד 30.11.2026").
2. `apps/worker/src/jobs/ocrInvoice.ts`: where the header is persisted. Store them as `ocr_payload.raw.printedTerms` and `ocr_payload.raw.printedDueDate`. Merge into `raw`; never replace it (the #171 rule: `scanGrId` and the consolidated fields must survive).
3. `packages/api/src/lib/dueDate.ts` (W2-03's file, read only): `computeDueDate(invoiceDate, terms, explicitDueDate?)`. Reuse its parser for the printed string. Don't fork a second parser.
4. `invoices.due_date` (`schema.ts:813`, read only). **Don't write it from OCR.** It stays the human-entered date. The printed date lives only in `raw.printedDueDate`, so a supplier's shorter printed date can never move the payment earlier (W2-03 §10 precedence: human, then agreed, then printed).

## 4. Acceptance criteria
1. **Read.** On the fixtures, printed "שוטף+60", "שוטף + 30", "net 45", "מזומן" and "לתשלום עד dd.mm.yyyy" are extracted into `raw.printedTerms` / `raw.printedDueDate`. An unreadable or absent string is `null`, never a guess.
2. **`termsCheck(agreedTerms, invoiceDate, printedTerms, printedDueDate)`** returns `ok | shorter | unknown` with both due dates.
   - `shorter` when the printed due date (stated directly, or computed from the printed terms) falls **before** the due date computed from the agreed terms by more than 0 days.
   - `unknown` when either side is missing or unparsed. That is neutral (T4), never an alarm.
3. **The flag** "תנאים קצרים מהמוסכם" appears in W2-03's pay run as a **warn** chip on the row, with both dates, e.g. "בחשבונית: 30.11 · מוסכם: 31.12". The pay run sorts by the **agreed** due date. The flag never adds ₪ to the leak, the hero, a claim or a letter.
4. **No regression:** `check:demo` is unchanged. The leak canary is green. OCR of an invoice with no printed terms behaves exactly as before.
