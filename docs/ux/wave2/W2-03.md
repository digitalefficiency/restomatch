# W2-03 · A pay run that ends: due dates, mark paid, holds, short-pay view
**One line:** the pay run becomes a due-date desk that empties. Every unpaid checked invoice is bucketed by when it is due. "סמן כשולם" records the payment without erasing a single claim. Held rows say who they wait for and how much of the total is actually disputed. This protects the money paid early, late or twice: MF-24950 (₪7,363.20, due 28.10) is hidden today, and a ₪100 dispute holds ₪4,513.15. ## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **`computeDueDate`.** `computeDueDate(invoiceDate, supplier.paymentTerms, explicitDueDate?)` lives in `lib/dueDate.ts`. It is pure and runs on the Asia/Jerusalem day.
   - An explicit or OCR `due_date` wins.
   - "שוטף+N", and variants such as "שוטף + 30" and "net+30", = **end of the invoice month + N days** (owner decision). Example: 28.09 + שוטף+30 → 30.10.
