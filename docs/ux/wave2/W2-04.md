# W2-04 · Approvals desk: invoice-level 3-pane, bulk, keys, history, notification landing
**One line:** a month-end queue of about 150 items becomes a desk you can clear. Cards group by invoice, bulk is capped and enforced by the server, the keys and the 3-pane mean evidence opens without leaving the queue, every decision leaves a trail, and an escalation reaches a person. A claim is born inside the decision, under the decider's name. This protects the ₪ that drifts when decisions are slow, invisible or attributed to whoever opened the recovery screen: ₪243.50 of undecided gaps reached supplier letters (decision 18), and a claim's 14-day clock today starts whenever someone looks. **Owner agent:** web-feature-engineer (tenant-security-warden is mandatory: new mutations, bulk, and the role scope). **Device:** both. **Effort:** L. **humanPR:** no (`approvals/engine.ts`, `outbox.ts` and `cron.ts` stay untouched).

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Grouping.** Cards group by invoice: "משקאות שטיינברג · ST-24108 · ₪104.80 בסיכון · 1 פער".
   - A group-level primary appears only when every line is non-block, the viewer can act on all of them, and the group's claimable ₪ ≤ ₪200 (owner decision: the cap is per invoice).
   - An info or non-claimable row reads "סמנו כנבדק" and is never "דרשו ₪X" (W1-07 verbs, decision→effect).
2. **Layout and keys.**
   - ≥1536: 3 panes (list 380 | document | decision 400). 1280–1535: 2 panes. Below 1280: the list plus a Peek/Sheet.
   - Keys per R-9 (`1/2/3 J/K X Z Enter O ?`), bound by `KeyboardEvent.code`, active only while the list has focus, never inside inputs.
   - `?sel=` is in the URL; Back keeps the position and the loaded pages.
   - ≥10 rows at default density and ≥15 at compact on 1440×900. Compact keeps a clamped WhyLine and never hides "why flagged".
3. **`approvals.decideMany`.** Input `{items: [{discrepancyId, expectedStatus}], decision, reasonCode?, clientMutationId}`. All-or-nothing, with per-item CONFLICT detail.
   - **The server refuses** any `block` row, and any invoice whose selected claimable ₪ > `BULK_APPROVE_CAP_AGOROT` (₪200 per invoice). This is enforced in the procedure, not only in BatchBar (attack test).
   - One audit row per item. A 10 s undo (`reopenMany`).
   - BatchBar reads "נבחרו N · ₪X". A web unit test asserts `DEFAULT_BULK_CAP_AGOROT === BULK_APPROVE_CAP_AGOROT`.
4. **One-tap channel.** `approvals.approve` accepts `channel?: 'desk'|'quick'`. `quick` (the future WhatsApp/push one-tap of H-05) is refused when the item's claimable ₪ > `ONE_TAP_APPROVE_CAP_AGOROT` (₪500) or its severity is `block`. This is a server test; H-05 only wires the button.
5. **Evidence.** "הצג ראיות" opens a Peek (480 px desktop, 85 vh Sheet on mobile) with the scan crop and the PO, קבלת סחורה and invoice lines, and the decision buttons inside.
   - The scan comes from W2-02's viewer when merged; until then a plain image from `/scans/<id>/file`. Evidence never needs a page navigation.
6. **History and escalation.**
   - A "היסטוריה" disclosure shows the last 3 audit rows (who, role, when, decision, note) via `approvals.history`.
   - "העבר לבעלים" opens a Sheet with note chips. The escalation writes `audit` + activity + a notification to each owner (concrete address or `notify()`), with ₪ and supplier.
   - The escalator keeps seeing it in "ממתין לבעלים · ₪X" (byRole from G-12).
   - Notifications respect the quiet hours (no alerts 23:00–06:00 or on Shabbat, at most 6 per person per day) by going only through `notify()` and the dispatcher. Never send in-process.
7. **The landing page.** `/dashboard/approvals/[invoiceId]` is the mobile landing for push and e-mail: a thumb card, a sticky 50/50 48 px pair, and the next card slides up.
   - Every notification link (matchInvoice e-mail, push and WhatsApp body, endOfDayReport) is `/dashboard/go?r=<restaurantId>&to=/dashboard/approvals?invoice=<id>`, or `?to=/dashboard/approvals` for the digest. The queue filters by `invoiceIds`.
8. **Claim at decision.** The claim is created inside the approve transaction, so `recovery_claims.created_at` = the decision time and `created_by` = the decider (test).
   - Undecided rows never get a claim (decision 18).
   - The verb's effect line names the channel by invoice state (decision 19): unpaid → "יקוזז בתשלום", paid → "דרישת זיכוי".
   - Opening `/dashboard/recovery` writes nothing (W2-10 asserts it; you don't break it).
9. **Cards** show the age and a due-date countdown (`DeadlineChip`: warn ≤7 days, danger only when late).
10. **Scope.** `myQueue` gains `scope: 'mine'|'all'` and returns `canAct` and `waitingOn` per row.
    - The bookkeeper reads `all` (owner decision: the bookkeeper reads everything) but decides only bookkeeper-routed rows.
    - Receiver and chef stay FORBIDDEN.
    - The output change is **additive**: the frozen mobile `apps/mobile/app/manager/index.tsx` still typechecks.
11. **Tiles.** Tiles and `EmptyQueue` read `queueSummary` = G-12 `awaitingDecision`. On the lab the owner sees 4 / ₪296.00 (unchanged), and the bookkeeper sees "ממתין לבעלים · 4 · ₪296.00", never "הכל טופל". Tiles use `MetricTile` at 0 dp; rows use 2 dp.
12. **Worker copy.** No "הפסד פוטנציאלי", no "חריגות" (use "פערים"), no baked `₪${}`. ₪ goes through the server money util or metadata. The approval e-mail's ₪ = Σ `claimableLines(computeInvoiceLeak(id))`. "דליפה" is never used here.
