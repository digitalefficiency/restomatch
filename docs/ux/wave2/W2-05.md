# W2-05 · Receiving 2: walk-in deliveries, multi-page capture, exceptions with evidence, outbox, desktop tower
**One line:** every real delivery enters the 3-way match: a supplier with no PO, page 2 of a חשבונית מרכזת, a short line with a photo and the driver's name, and a count made with no signal. Nothing is lost on reload, and the owner gets a desk view of קבלת סחורה. This protects the "billed 10, arrived 8" ₪24-per-line catch at the door, and the ₪675–1,125/month of PO-less spend at ₪150k that is invisible today (FG-11, receiving-04). **Owner agent:** ocr-procurement-engineer (tenant-security-warden is mandatory: new procedures, storage paths, offline replay). **Device:** both. **Effort:** L. ## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Count truth (server).** `markGrLine` takes `outcome` and `clientMutationId`. Its `UPDATE … WHERE` carries the expected prior state, and a replay of an identical payload returns the row with no second audit row.
   - `submitReceipt` runs in one transaction under `lockReceipt` with `withIdempotency`. With uncounted lines it throws `PRECONDITION_FAILED {uncounted_lines: n}`, unless `closeAs: 'partial'`, which closes as `partial` and records the "לא נספר" line ids.
   - "חסר" is stored 0/0/`missing` and never appears as rejected.
   - **Decision 20 holds:** "הכל הגיע" still asks for the 2-line spot check before the bulk confirm and marks every other line "לא נספר" (W1-06 behaviour). A server test asserts that a `bulk_as_ordered` close records the uncounted line ids and never stores them as counted.
2. **Resume.** `receiving.resumeState({poId})` returns grId, status, marks and `{invoiceId, status} | null`. A reload, or another phone, lands on review with no re-shoot and **no second OCR job** (test).
   - A second scan on the same receipt asks "עמוד נוסף / חשבונית אחרת". "עמוד נוסף" composes it into the same document.
3. **Walk-in.** "ספק הגיע בלי הזמנה" (a 56 px FAB plus the empty-state CTA) → supplier chips → that supplier's recent items → Delivery Check → GR plus a retro PO (`source='manual'`, note "קבלה ללא הזמנה", `source_ref = walk_in:<grId>`, draft).
   - Expected price: contract > the restaurant's own last price > list, never the invoice (decision 21). The PO is never sent to the supplier.
   - Until a manager runs `confirmWalkIn` (a receiver gets FORBIDDEN), the invoice stays UNORDERED_ARRIVAL with the owner. After it, the PO becomes matchable and one re-match is enqueued.
   - "לא הגיע" on a due PO records the fact (audit + manager notified). The manager cancels or reschedules in orders (W2-06's UI).
4. **Multi-page capture.**
   - A page loop ("+ עמוד" / "סיימתי · שלחו 3 עמ׳") with a thumbnail strip (retake, delete, reorder).
   - Each page is a JPEG (webp converted) uploaded direct to storage via `scans.uploadUrl`, with a `clientPageId` under one `clientDocumentId`, behind a determinate XHR progress bar.
   - `scans.composeDocument` calls G-09 → one PDF, one invoice, one OCR job. `invoice_scans` gets the invoice uuid and `page_count = 3`.
   - Offline pages queue as one outbox item per page.
5. **Document chip:** חשבונית מס / תעודת משלוח / חשבונית זיכוי, defaulted from the supplier's history. A delivery note is stored on the GR (photos + `ת.מ. <n>` in `goods_receipts.notes` until G-03's column; see §8), never as an invoice.
6. **Exceptions.** ✗ on a line opens a Sheet:
   - reason chips (חסר / פגום / לא לפי ההזמנה / טמפרטורה / אחר), `QtyKeypad`, a 48 px rear-camera photo saved as a **storage path** under `<restaurantId>/` in `gr_lines.photos`, and a note;
   - Finish asks "זיכוי / החלפה" and the driver's name, with an optional signature canvas saved as a storage path in `signature_url`;
   - a short or rejected line → `emit('receipt.closed_with_exceptions')` → each manager and owner gets a notification at a concrete address, with the supplier and line. It goes only through the dispatcher (quiet hours: none 23:00–06:00 or on Shabbat, ≤6 per person per day).
7. **Dock slip.** It is offered only after the count, full screen, ≥16 px text. It lists counted facts (arrived / short / rejected / not ordered).
   - **Owner decisions win over today's slip:** no ₪ for the receiver or the chef, and no undecided price gap on a slip that goes to the supplier (decision 18). Price gaps go to the owner's decision. Say so in the PR body.
   - Each share is recorded (audit now; activity `event` + `metadata.kind='receipt.slip_shared'` after G-03).
8. **Outbox.** `lib/pwa/outbox.ts` (IndexedDB) has `registerOutboxKind(kind, {send, idempotencyKey, classify})` and the kinds `grLine`, `submitReceipt`, `photo`, `slipShared`.
   - Backoff 2/4/8…60 s. A 401 or a network drop costs no attempt (`classifyFailure`).
   - A header chip "⟳ 3 ממתינים" opens per-item status. W1-06's localStorage rows (`rm-rcv-lines:*`, `rm-rcv-unordered:*`) migrate with zero loss.
   - Decision 22: a colleague's same-restaurant items upload with "צולם על ידי"; another restaurant's wait, and only the owner deletes them. Sign-out never wipes silently.
   - Airplane-mode e2e: a 12-line receipt survives a reload and syncs **exactly once** (1 row per line, 1 submit audit).
9. **Offline shell.** The SW caches `/_next/static` cache-first plus a field shell route. Today's POs are precached, so `/dashboard/receiving/[poId]` renders with zero bars. The snapshot is purged on sign-out. `receiverSw.test.ts` is extended.
10. **Desk view (≥1280).** A receipts `DataTable`:
    - columns: date navigation, state from `deliveryState`, receiver, invoice status, slip sent, plus ₪ ordered and exceptions ₪ **only for owner, manager and bookkeeper** (server-redacted: receiver and chef get no ₪ fields);
    - numbers right-aligned (DataTable's `NUM_ALIGN`); polls every 30 s while visible;
    - a row opens a `SplitPane` (image | lines ordered/billed/received/Δ₪ | evidence + timeline).
    - Lines use one stable interim sort (category, then name), shared by the PO, receipt and invoice views, until H-03's `line_no`.
11. **Board.** `todayExpectations` returns every open commitment by `deliveryState`. A late PO stays until it is received, cancelled or rescheduled; a received one never shows as late.
12. **Roles.**
    - The bookkeeper corrects invoice header/lines (`invoice.correct`) in place.
    - The chef receives only when the owner turns the setting on (default off; the mechanism is in §8). Chilled lines take an optional °C.
    - No ₪ for the receiver or the chef on any new procedure (server test).
13. **Copy:** קבלת סחורה, פער, חשבונית מרכזת. No "דליפה". `receiverLangs` overlays carry every new verb. he/en parity.
