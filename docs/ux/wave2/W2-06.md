# W2-06 · Orders 2: order guide, cut-off board, send from my WhatsApp, chef orders, supplier changes
**One line:** a routine 10-line order takes ≤ 9 taps and ≤ 45 s on the phone. It starts from an order guide built from the last 8 POs. The manager or the chef sends it from their own WhatsApp, and the server saves it at every line. The PO exists before the goods arrive, so the match has three documents, not two. An order placed by WhatsApp or phone outside RestoMatch makes every QTY_SHORT/PRICE_HIGHER ₪ on that delivery invisible (orders-01 P0, orders-07 P0, functional-flows-07 P0). There is also one expected price, contract > the restaurant's own last price > list, never the invoice, so "מחיר גבוה" means the same ₪ on every order path. **Owner agent:** web-feature-engineer (tenant-security-warden **mandatory**: capabilities and 9 new procedures; leak-economist signs `resolveExpectedPrice`). **Device:** phone (the desktop list is in scope too). **Effort:** L. **humanPR:** no.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
The owner decisions win where the plan differs; each case is marked **[owner]**.
1. **Guide.** `orders.orderGuide({supplierId})` (`can('order.draft')`):
   - rows come from this supplier's last 8 POs (no drafts, cancelled or walk-ins), items in ≥ 2 of the 8;
   - `usualQty` = the median of the last 4, plus `lastQty`; grouped by category, then storage area;
   - a ghost "בד״כ 5", and "כמו בפעם הקודמת" with undo;
   - 48 px `QuantityInput` steppers and `QtyKeypad` for weighed units;
   - **no line ever starts at qty 1**: a searched item starts empty, with the usual qty as its ghost;
   - a routine 10-line order: ≤ 9 taps and ≤ 45 s with no keyboard (e2e timer).
2. **Board.** `orders.supplierBoard()` is one batched query:
   - 72 px rows sorted by next cut-off, with a "היום" group (orders-19);
   - chips "נסגר בעוד 2:15": warn ≤ 3 h, danger ≤ 30 min. The danger chip is the only T2;
   - "הזמנה אחרונה: לפני 3 ימים · ₪875", with the ₪ dropped without `price.view`. The SENT step offers "הבא בתור".
3. **Send from my WhatsApp.** `orders.markSentManually({poId, clientMutationId})` (`can('order.send')` + `canOn`):
   - draft → `sent`, `sentChannel 'whatsapp'`, **no outbox row**, idempotent. The list reads "נשלחה ידנית בוואטסאפ";
   - the exact text is on the client before the tap. Reuse `documentPreview`, or add an idempotent `prepareManualSend` that reuses the unexpired confirm link. Call it on the explicit "מהוואטסאפ שלי" choice, never on page open;
   - the tap opens `wa.me` **synchronously**, then calls the mutation;
   - it is the default channel when the plan lacks `whatsapp_alerts` and the supplier has a WhatsApp number.
4. **The chef orders directly, with no approval [owner].** This overrides plan acceptance 4 (`createDraftRequest`, "שלח למנהל לאישור") and GAP G-07 §5; say so in the PR.
   - The chef drafts, edits and sends their own orders, and can't touch a manager's draft.
   - The manager's list gets a "טיוטות (N)" view of every unsent draft, author named, with one-tap send.
   - `routes/orders.ts` adds `chef` to `roles` only. No bar slot: the chef bar is asserted in `W1-03.spec.ts:24`, and that is W2-08's call.
   - **The chef and the receiver see no ₪ [owner, decision 6].** `redactPrices` on `list`, `get`, `orderGuide`, `supplierBoard`, `documentPreview` and `guardrail`. The WhatsApp/PDF text the chef opens has no prices, even when the restaurant's document includes them. No Σ footer and no ₪ column.
   - A WhatsApp share never 404s. As chef → a captured draft. As receiver → an in-shell T4 page that keeps the text, with "העבירו למנהל" (share/copy). Whether the receiver may draft is open (G-07 §7); ask the orchestrator.
5. **Detail.**
   - The primary follows the state: draft → "שלחו"; sent/confirmed → "קבלת סחורה" (links to `/dashboard/receiving/<poId>`); received with a gap → "ראו פערים · ₪X" (only with `money.view`).
   - A 3-way table הוזמן · התקבל · חויב · פער ₪, from the GR lines plus `match_runs.po_id`. Its ₪ columns are hidden without `money.view`, and numbers are right-aligned **[owner]**.
   - The supplier's answer as a diff, "הספק שינה 2 שורות", from `supplierResponseDiff`. Accepting it into the expectation is H-04/H-08.
   - A timeline "נשלח 09:00 · ענה 09:40", and W2-05's "לא הגיע" fact with cancel/reschedule.
   - `orders.addOrderLine` and `productId` on `updateOrderLine` (drafts), with a suggestion and its basis via `decideLink`.
   - `orders.amendSent` before `nextOrderCutoffAt` (refused once a GR exists) produces "תיקון להזמנה #F8E05686: +2 ק״ג עגבניות", which sends only on a click.
6. **List.**
   - Desktop: a `DataTable` with `ViewTabs` and live counts (דורש טיפול · מגיע היום · באיחור · טיוטות · השבוע); search by supplier, #ref or product; a Σ footer equal to the visible rows; a supplier-answer column and filter (אושר · אושר עם 2 שינויים · ממתין לספק).
   - Bulk send: `orders.sendMany({items:[{poId, expectedStatus:'draft'}], channel})`, all-or-nothing with per-item CONFLICT.
   - Phone: day groups היום/מחר/השבוע, relative dates, one inline action per state, a 56 px FAB in the thumb zone, no `font-mono` on money.
   - Capture: clipboard paste, inline mapping with a suggestion and its basis, a sticky confirm.
   - The orders-25 checkbox moves into a ⋯ "הגדרות מסמך ההזמנה" sheet with an explicit save.
7. **Server draft.** `orders.saveDraft({draftId?, supplierId, lines, deliveryDate, version, clientMutationId})` and `orders.openDraft({supplierId})`:
   - the version is `xmin`; one open draft per (restaurant, supplier, author) under `pg_advisory_xact_lock`; lock the PO and check `NOT EXISTS goods_receipts` before replacing lines;
   - a stale version → CONFLICT naming who and when; a replay returns the recorded result;
   - after a reload, "המשך מהטיוטה (3 פריטים)" comes from the server, even in another browser of the same user;
   - a supplier switch keeps both drafts. The sessionStorage mirror stays only as the offline fallback.
8. **Expected price.** `resolveExpectedPrice` = contract (in force today, converted to the line's unit) > the last restaurant-authored, explicitly priced PO line (never imported, never walk-in, carries `asOf`, ignored when the list price was updated after it) > list. **Never the invoice.**
   - "Explicitly priced": no column says whether a price was typed or prefilled. Use lines of **sent** POs with source `manual|recurring` only, and propose `po_lines.price_source` for H-04 in the PR.
   - `createDraft`, `captureOrderDraft`, reorder and (W2-05's) walk-in give the same price for the same input (test).
9. **`placeOrder`** refuses a PO that has any `goods_receipts` (CONFLICT). It builds the target through a typed address (G-06: the supplier's email or E.164 WhatsApp) and keeps dedupe `po:<id>:placed`, so no `role:` target is possible. The platform WhatsApp stays free text and plan-gated until H-05.
10. **Money, inputs, copy:**
    - `<Money>` everywhere; "1,250" = ₪1,250 via `parseDecimalInput` (W1-11's parsers until G-13);
    - in the he PDF and text, ₪ sits left of the first digit (`visualOrder` after G-13);
    - copy: "קבלת סחורה", "פער"; never "דליפה" here; plural imperatives; no "1.5%" anywhere **[owner]**.
