## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Audited denominator.**
   - `inventory.setSales({countId, foodSalesExVat})` runs on `managerProcedure` (owner, manager), with `recordAudit('inventory.sales_set', before, after)`. It is allowed after close.
   - `setMeta` no longer accepts `foodSalesExVat` (a test proves the chef can't set it by any route).
   - PeriodCard shows "הוזן ע״י <name> · <date>" from the audit row.
   - **Owner decision wins:** the chef sees food cost **% only**: no sales, purchase or stock ₪ (the plan said "sales read-only or %").
2. **One labelled stock figure.**
   - `history` and `get` return `foodValueAgorot` alongside `totalValueAgorot`.
   - Every surface reads "מלאי מזון ₪9,490.66 · כולל בר ומשקאות ₪13,474.xx", both at 2 dp in that sentence. Tiles stay 0 dp (DoD-4).
   - Bar lines carry "לא נכנס לעלות המזון". The lab values themselves don't change.
3. **No ₪ for the chef, server-side.** For roles without `money.view`, `current`, `get`, `start` and `complete` return no `unitCost`, `valueAgorot`, `priceSource` or `totalValueAgorot` keys (test on the JSON, not on the DOM).
4. **Field shell** (`data-mode="field"` on `/count`; the bar already steps aside):
   - a sticky area header "מקרר · 7/17" (plus " · ₪1,240" for money viewers only) and an area chip bar with completion rings;
   - Enter/Next → the next uncounted qty (`scrollIntoView` center), and at the end of an area → the next area. 0 navigation taps per line, and focus never lands on a select;
   - the area select leaves the row for a "סידור אזורים" mode;
   - a "חפש בספירה" field at the top;
   - the date becomes a chip "תאריך: היום, 25.10.2026 · שינוי" opening a he-IL sheet with "המחירים יחושבו לפי התאריך הזה";
   - a 56 px qty field with 24 px tabular digits, ≥48 px targets, 14 px ink units via `UnitText`, and the price source untruncated with `<bdi>` around the invoice number;
   - lines keep `loadLines`' area order; the shelf order is H-04.
5. **Last time + guard.**
   - Each line returns `prevQty`, `prevUnit` and `receivedSince` (net, counted lines of closed receipts), shown muted as "בפעם הקודמת 8 · התקבלו 6" and never prefilled.
   - An instant client guard fires at >2× or <50 % of (prev + received), or >₪2,000 on a line:
     - for money viewers: "999 תבניות = ₪33,966 — נכון?";
     - for the chef: the same check from a server `valueFlag`, with copy without ₪.
   - Finish is blocked until every flag is confirmed, and the panel lists the flags as jump links. `complete`'s audit lists the confirmed flags.
6. **Hand-off.** The chef's primary "סיימתי לספור · שלחו לסגירה" calls `inventory.submitForClose`:
   - an audit row `inventory.count_submitted`, and a notification to each manager/owner at a concrete address, through the dispatcher only (no alerts 23:00–06:00 or on Shabbat, ≤6 per person per day);
   - `current` exposes `submittedAt`/`submittedBy` for W2-08's card "ספירה מוכנה לסגירה · 50/52", with ₪ only for money viewers. The interim state comes from the latest audit row (status column = H-04).
7. **Discard and amend.**
   - Discard opens a `ConfirmDialog` naming the scope ("למחוק ספירה 25.10 עם 37 כמויות?"). Only the count's starter or a manager+ may discard (server).
   - Amend opens a `Sheet` with a decimal qty, reason chips (טעות הקלדה / יחידה שגויה / פריט נשכח / אחר) and the ₪ before → after for money viewers. A short reason shows an inline error, never a silent abort.
   - The action label is "תיקון כמות" / "Fix quantity".
8. **Outbox.**
   - Line edits are the `inventoryLines` kind on W2-05's core. The old `pendingStore` localStorage rows migrate with zero loss.
   - A T4 chip "↑ 3 ממתינים · נשמר במכשיר" retries on `online` and on visibilitychange→visible with 2/4/8…60 s backoff. Finish shows the flush reason inline.
   - Attribution follows decision 22 (a same-restaurant colleague's edits upload as "נספר על ידי <name>").
   - Airplane-mode e2e: a 20-line count survives a reload and syncs **exactly once** (the wave-2 gate).
9. **Closed count.** A `DataTable`: item | area | qty | unit cost | value | Δ qty / Δ₪ vs the previous count.
   - Area subtotals; chips "לא נספר (N)" (listing them), "בלי מחיר" and "שינוי חריג".
   - Amend sits in the row menu. Numbers are right-aligned (DataTable `NUM_ALIGN`). `MobileCardList` on phone.
10. **Bookkeeper read-only (owner decision).**
    - The `inventory` route roles gain `bookkeeper`.
    - `history`, `get` (completed counts only), `period` and `exportCsv` move to a local reader tier (`memberProcedure` + a roles check: owner, manager, chef, bookkeeper).

11. **Data truth** (a signed ₪ change).
