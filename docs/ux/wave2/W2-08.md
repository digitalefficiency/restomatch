## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **`tasks.mine({role?})`** is computed (no table). It returns `{type, entityId, title, ilsAgorot|null, tier T1–T4, due, ageDays, verb, deepLink, scope: 'mine'|'others'}`.
   - Types: `order_due`, `supplier_change`, `delivery_due`, `delivery_late`, `receipt_exception`, `doc_review`, `decision`, `waiting_on_others`, `consolidated_link`, `claim_to_send`, `supplier_disputed`, `credit_to_match`, `allocation_missing`, `payment_due`, `close_step`.
   - **The owner decision overrides the plan:** the chef orders directly, so `order_request` is not an approval task. The manager sees a T4 "השף הזמין" item in the bell's news only.
   - `claim_to_send` covers decided gaps only (decision 18).
   - ₪ comes from G-12's `claimableLines` rule. Ranked by ₪ × urgency: block > due today > age. Scoped by G-07 `VISIBLE_TO`.
   - `ilsAgorot` is `null` **on the server** for roles without `money.view` (receiver, chef).
2. **Homes** render "מחכה לך" from `tasks.mine`: at most 3 rows plus "עוד N ←". Each row has a solid badge word, ₪, count, age and one verb.
   - "הכל טופל" (T3) only when the **whole restaurant** has nothing waiting. Otherwise: "אצלך הכל טופל · 4 ממתינים אצל הבעלים · ₪296".
   - The PLACEHOLDER export row is removed.
   - At 375×667 the hero ₪ and the first row are both above the fold.
3. **`nav.badges`** is one batched query (staleTime 30 s, refetch on focus and every 60 s). It feeds the sidebar, the rail dot, the bottom bar and the "עוד" sheet.
   - ₪ in `<Money>` (0 dp) on money items: approvals, claims to send, pay run. Counts on capture items: receiving today/late, count progress, intake.
   - Danger only with a block. Hidden at 0. Capped at `99+`.
   - aria-label: "אישורים, 4 פריטים, ‏296 ₪ בסיכון".
   - Receiver and chef get counts only.
   - The price-alert badge is neutral below W2-12's materiality (≥3 % and ≥₪50/month).
4. **The bell** is a 360 px `Popover` at ≥1024 and a `Sheet` on the phone, with the same source plus T3 news.
   - Items deep-link to the exact card (`?inv=`, `#id`), never to the home.
   - "Seen" lives in localStorage (try/catch; the bell renders without it). `navigator.setAppBadge(count)` where supported.
   - No OS notification and no sound: quiet hours 23:00–06:00 and Shabbat, and the 6/day cap, are H-05.
5. **The hero** uses `LeakHero` with `<Money context="hero">` (0 dp), and tiles use `MetricTile`.
   - No `font-mono`, `text-[11px]` or glow. `ReturnedTile` becomes a T3 `MetricTile`.
   - The secondary KPIs fold into "35 נבדקו · 22 נקיות · 2 ממתינות ⓘ".
   - The price teaser is W2-12's `PriceAlertsTeaser` (top alert with ≈₪/month, T2 only at ≥3 % and ≥₪50). You place it; you don't re-implement it.
   - No "1.5% target" anywhere. The % of food spend shows with its base only.
6. **The in-shell 404** calls `nav.whereIs({kind: invoice|po|supplier|claim|count, id})`.
   - Only for an id in another restaurant **the viewer is a member of**, it shows "שייך ל‹סניף› · פתחו שם" → `/dashboard/go?r=&to=`.
   - "Foreign tenant" and "does not exist" give the identical response (no existence oracle).
7. **Footer and states:** the footer renders G-11's `LegalLinks` (keep `SUPPORT_LINKS` until it merges). A vatAtRisk failure is an in-shell error with retry, never a missing card. Unknown is never ₪0.
8. **Registry and focus:** a test asserts that every role in each `RouteDef.roles` holds the capability of the route's primary read procedure (G-07 `CAP`). The focus ring uses `--c-focus`.
9. **Demo invariants (lab, 25.10).** These match on the home row, the badge, the bell and `/dashboard/approvals`:
