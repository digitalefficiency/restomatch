## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Evidence fields.** `LeakBreakdownLine` gains these fields, additive only:
   - `toleranceUsed`, `qtyBilled`, `unit`, `unitPriceBilled`, `unitPriceExpected`;
   - `poNumber`, `deliveryNoteNumber` (GR);
   - `expectedSource: 'po'|'contract'|'p90'|'receipt'|'invoiceTotal'|'vat'` and `expectedAsOf`.
   The hero, every breakdown sum and the canary stay exactly as they are (a charts test pins the lab ₪759.54).
2. **Evidence math.** `packages/api/src/leaks/evidence.ts` recomputes each line with the engine's `mulIls`/`unitConversionFactor`.
   - If the result ≠ |Δ| to the agora, the line shows "פירוט לא זמין".
   - The source label is truthful: "בהזמנה PO-123 (14.10)" vs "חציון 90 יום".
   - A capped line adds "מוגבל לסכום החשבונית".
3. **One why formatter,** `flags/why.ts` `whySegments(facts, labels)`, hoisted from `approvals/vocab.ts:247`. It feeds `WhyLine` on breakdown lines, proof findings and the supplier leak card, and W2-10 (letters) and W2-04 (approvals) adopt it.
   - Copy: "חויב ₪149.00 × 10 ק״ג · בהזמנה ₪139.00 · הפרש ₪100.00 · סף ±2% או ₪5".
   - Compact density keeps one line (clamped, never dropped). Line ₪ has 2 dp; unit prices up to 4.
4. **The drill contract.** `DrillLink` renders `<a data-drill data-drill-total={agorot}>` with an accessible name like "נתפס לפני תשלום ₪296 — פתחו 4 שורות".
   - It sits on every hero part (W2-08 places it), KPI tile, bar, heatmap tile (a button) and report row.
   - The landing header echoes the same ₪ from the same procedure.
   - An e2e clicks every `[data-drill]` on the leak routes and asserts the landing total equals the clicked total to the agora. On a later change: a T4 "המספר התעדכן מאז".
5. **The hub.** Tabs: סקירה · פירוק · הוכחה · מחירים שעלו · דוח. **The owner's terminology overrides the plan's "מגמת מחירים"** (price creep is "מחירים שעלו").
   - Every sub-page's up-link goes to `/dashboard/leaks`.
   - Every old URL still resolves: 200, or a 308 if you move it.
   - `m` and `month` 308 to `p`/`from`/`to` (next.config `redirects()` with `has`), keeping the other params.
   - **Scope note:** the ia-nav-12 phase-2 moves of `/leaks/claims` and `/recovery` are **not** in wave 2 (W2-10 owns those folders).
6. **The breakdown** is a dense, sortable DataTable (aria-sort, ₪ descending by default) with a 480 px Peek on Space. Two groups (decision 19 wording; a line is never in both):
   - "עוד לא שולם — לקזז לפני תשלום · ₪296.00";
   - "שולם — לדרישת זיכוי · ₪463.54".
   The CTA follows the invoice state:
   - unpaid → "החליטו על 2 פערים · ₪100.00" → `/dashboard/approvals?inv=<id>`;
   - paid → "לדרישת הזיכוי" → `/dashboard/leaks/claims?sup=<id>`;
   - undecided gaps read "החליטו", never "לדרישה" (decision 18).
   Group and type totals sum the capped amounts. Past LIMIT, an "אחר" row keeps total = rows.
7. **Periods.** `period.ts` `resolveWindow(view, now, tz)`:
   - while the month runs: current + same-length previous ("1–25 באוק׳ מול 1–25 בספט׳"); after close: full vs full;
   - Asia/Jerusalem, with coverage (invoices checked per window); the % is hidden when coverage differs materially.
   `computeLeakComparison` runs on `boundsCte`. `leakState` adds `partial` ("נבדקו 1 מתוך 13").
8. **Drivers.** `leakDrivers({view, compare})` returns the top 3 contributors to Δ (supplier, product, type) in agorot, with an "אחר" rest. Up to 3 `DrillLink` chips sit under the hero delta and on the report ("+₪74 קצביית הכרם · אנטריקוט +7.2%"). Up is danger, down is green.
9. **Exports.** The report exports as a 3-sheet XLSX (built with exceljs in `packages/api`; sheets type, supplier and מחירים שעלו) plus a CSV per sheet. The breakdown CSV is line-level with the WhyLine fields: invoice, line, qty, unit, billed, expected, source, PO, ת״מ, Δ, tolerance, status. CSV totals equal the screen.
10. **Phone** (<640): hero + up to 3 act-now cards + the top 5 rows with a 56 px thumb CTA. Tables become `MobileCardList` with ₪ at the line end. No horizontal scroll.
11. **Tiers.** `owner.kpis` money fields go only to roles with `money.view` (receiver and chef get counts; the tier flip itself is H-11). `owner.leakReport` admits the bookkeeper (finance tier).
12. **Design system:**
    - `<Money>`/`MetricTile`; no `font-mono`, no uppercase or tracking on Hebrew, a 12 px floor, tokens instead of hex;
    - `NUM_ALIGN` right-aligns numbers in both locales (owner decision);
    - `LeakHeatmap` drops `dir="ltr"`;
    - "קו" only for time groupings; no RTL mirror on the time axis (left = oldest);
    - each chart has an aria summary sentence and a "הצגה כטבלה" link.
13. **History.** Each breakdown line has a "היסטוריה" disclosure: the latest 3 entries (who + role chip, verb, before→after, dd.MM.yyyy HH:mm Asia/Jerusalem). `report/page.tsx:101` becomes an error state.
