## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Claims at decision.** `materializeClaims` runs inside the decision transaction, with `created_by` = the decider and `claimed_at` = the decision time. It is idempotent per discrepancy, and it is registered as the G-05 `claim` effect for `discrepancy.decided(claim)`.
   - `recovery.summary`/`list` write nothing: a test compares row counts before and after a GET.
   - A paid invoice keeps its claims, and "נתפס" is measured at decision time (owner decision).
2. **The letter** is a `claim_letters` row, one draft per supplier, built only from that supplier's `claimed` claims. Never from `leakBreakdown` sums, and never from an undecided gap (decision 18).
   - **The owner decision overrides the plan's "one draft merges unpaid and paid lines".** Two sections, one channel per state (decision 19):
     - "לקיזוז לפני תשלום" (unpaid; W2-03 records the short-pay as a `payment_deductions` row);
     - "לזיכוי" (paid).
   - Each claim sits in exactly one section of exactly one letter, and Σ letter = Σ its claims to the agora.
   - Evidence is auto-attached as links to the originals: the delivery-note photo, the invoice crop, the contract price.
3. **Preview and send.**
   - The preview shows exactly what the supplier gets: print/PDF, WhatsApp text and mail body, all from one `content_snapshot`.
   - Send is a wa.me or mailto hand-off with a 10 s "בטל שליחה". `sendLetter` marks the letter `sent` and its claims `letter_sent` **only on confirm**.
   - `unsendLetter` reverses within N minutes (guarded) and returns a version CONFLICT on a stale screen.
   - No server send (H-06).
4. **Copy (polite; uses "פער" and "דרישת זיכוי").**
   - Line: "{פריט} · {כמות} {יח׳} · חויב ₪{billed} ליח׳ במקום ₪{agreed} שסוכם (הזמנה {po}, ת״מ {dn}, {date}) = ₪{delta}".
   - "שסוכם" only for a PO or contract source. A p90 basis reads "במקום ₪{p90} (חציון 90 יום)".
   - Shortage: "הוזמן {x}, התקבל {y} (ת״מ {dn} חתומה)".
   - Subject: "הנדון: דרישת זיכוי — פערים בחשבוניות {month}". The current `leaks.claims.subject` already complies (D-3).
   - Closing: "נבקש חשבונית זיכוי בסך ₪{total} לפני מע״מ, או חשבונית מתוקנת, עד {date}."
   - All text comes from W2-09's `whySegments`, with no local math.
5. **Desk.** A split view:
   - left: suppliers ranked by owed ₪ × days overdue;
   - right: the dossier, grouped by next action — לשליחה · ממתין לספק · במחלוקת · זוכה — לאשר קיזוז — each with a count and ₪ (2 dp).
   Fully credited ₪0-owed claims never lead the open list. URL state `?tab=&s=` (Back- and refresh-stable). Sortable with aria-sort, plus a supplier chip.
6. **Server-side next steps.** `recovery.list` returns `next[]` per claim from the server machine; the client `NEXT` in `logic.ts` is deleted (its test cases move to a server test, unweakened).
7. **History.** `recovery.history` returns the actor's display name and membership role. The 480 px Peek shows:
   - the claim math (WhyLine), invoice links and dock evidence;
   - the timeline, in dd.MM.yyyy HH:mm Asia/Jerusalem.
   Exports include the history.
8. **Credit form.**
   - A "כולל מע״מ / לפני מע״מ" SegmentedControl with **no default**; submit is disabled until one is chosen.
   - The field starts empty, labelled "הסכום כפי שמופיע בתעודה"; no claim is pre-selected.
   - Conversion happens on the server, in agorot, half-up, at `suppliers.vat_rate ?? restaurants.vat_rate` (never the engine's 0.17). Example: ₪118.00 incl. at 0.18 → ₪100.00.
   - A live "נבחר ₪X מתוך ₪Y · הפרש ₪Z" shows ✓ at 0.
   - Input goes through `parseDecimalInput(raw,'ils')` (interim `parseIlsInput`): "1,250" = ₪1,250.00.
   - The unallocated remainder shows on the supplier row (computed; no column).
9. **Auto-advance and bulk.**
   - A credited claim whose credit line sits on an invoice that becomes paid moves to `recovered` with "קוזז בחשבונית X ששולמה 12.11". It is driven by the G-05 paid event; test it with a fixture until W2-03's `markPaid` ships.
   - Bulk "אשרו קיזוז/החזר" goes through a ConfirmDialog that names the ₪ and the supplier, and it blocks or asks again when outstanding > 0.
   - Terminal edges need a reason.
10. **Transitions** take `items[{claimId, expectedStatus}]`, all-or-nothing, with a per-item CONFLICT. Rows update optimistically with an inline error and "נסו שוב", with no page refresh. Zero writes on read.
11. **Period, export, pages.**
    - A period filter on the claimed, credited and recovered dates via W2-09's resolver; `returnedInMonth` uses it.
    - CSV + XLSX (exceljs in `packages/api/src/recovery/export.ts`), including the history.
    - A per-supplier printable "דף דרישות" (browser PDF).
    - Pagination "200 מתוך 340", never a silent truncation. The tiles count everything.
12. **Reminders (owner decision 16).** An overdue reminder is a per-supplier **draft** ("תזכורת לספק · 3 דרישות · ₪451.00") that opens wa.me or mailto **only on a click**. Nothing goes to a supplier automatically. The managers' daily digest stays, and `reminders.ts` stops calling `syncClaims`.
13. **Parity** (`recovery-parity.test.ts`; W2-03's `payrun-parity.test.ts` asserts the pay-run side):
    - Σ letters = Σ claims on the lab month and the canary fixtures (read-only);
    - no discrepancy appears in two letters;
    - attach a signed before/after (lab: ₪846.88 asked today vs ₪759.54 leak).
14. **States:** the receiver and chef never reach claims or recovery (finance tier). `<Money>`, numbers right-aligned in both locales, a layout-true `loading.tsx`, and in-shell errors.
