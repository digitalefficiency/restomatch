## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **The overview** is the default tab. Tabs go down to 5: סקירה · מחירים · הזמנות · כרטסת · פרטים.
   - `catalog` and `contract` → מחירים; `schedule` → פרטים#ימים-ושעות; `leak` → סקירה + the leak card. Every old `?tab=` keeps resolving.
   - **The header:**
     - one "דליפה · 90 יום" ₪ from the same source as the leak card (`owner.leakGrouped({groupBy:'supplier'})` over `leakCardWindow`), 0 dp in `<Money>`;
     - `owner.leaks({limit:100})` is dropped;
     - a tertiary "איך הגענו ל-₪X?";
     - [WhatsApp] [חיוג] via `waLink`/`telLink`, and the primary [הזמנה חדשה] → `/dashboard/orders/new?supplierId=`.
   - **The act-now row** has up to 3 ₪-ranked cards: awaiting decision (→ `approvals?sup=`), open to claim (→ `leaks/claims?sup=`), over contract (W2-12).
   - **The rhythm line:** "הזמנה עד ב׳ 12:00 (בעוד 2:10) · משלוח ג׳ 09:30" (Asia/Jerusalem, 24 h).
   - A 6-month spend sparkline with an aria sentence, the price movers with ₪/month and their basis, and the last 5 invoices.
2. **The vendor table (≥1024)** is a DataTable with these columns: ספק · רכש החודש ₪ · דליפה ₪ (90 יום, desc by default) · מעל חוזה ₪ · פתוח לדרישה ₪ · משלוח הבא · תנאי תשלום.
   - A totals row = Σ the rows. The split detail uses `SplitPane`.
   - The scorecards fold into columns ("בדיקות חשבונית", never "match runs").
   - One server query (`suppliers.table`), with no per-row fetch.
3. **Phone.** Cards are sorted by "מגיע היום", then the soonest cut-off, then the rest. Each card has:
   - a status chip;
   - "פתוח לדרישה ₪X" when > 0;
   - 48 px [WhatsApp] [חיוג] [הזמנה].
   A tap opens an 85 vh `Sheet` overview, not the form. Phone numbers, ח״פ and e-mails sit in `<Token>`/`<bdi>` as tel:/wa.me/mailto links; spell it "ח״פ".
4. **The ledger.**
   - Rows and columns: 36 px rows, a totals row and column, sortable.
   - Range: `?from=&to=` (YYYY-MM, up to 24 months; default 6).
   - Cells: invoiced ₪ in 2 dp, right-aligned in both locales. Show the leak only when > 0, and chips only for exceptions. Blank means no activity.
   - "כל התעודות חויבו" replaces "מותאם", and the current month has the eyebrow "פתוח · עד 25.10".
   - A cell opens a 520 px drawer (`SupplierLedgerTable`, `?sup=&m=`) **inside** `/dashboard/suppliers/ledger`, with no bounce to the hub for the bookkeeper.
   - The CSV has all per-supplier columns: notes, invoiced, leak, credits asked, paid, open balance.

   - The account-card link opens W2-14's mapping.
   - **The receiver gets no supplier ledger:**

6. **Forms.**
   - **ח״פ:** 9 digits plus the Israeli check digit (weights 1,2,1,2…; the sum of digits mod 10 = 0). Messages: "ח״פ צריך 9 ספרות" / "ח״פ לא תקין — בדקו את הספרות". A duplicate warning: "ספק עם ח״פ זה כבר קיים — לפתוח אותו?".
