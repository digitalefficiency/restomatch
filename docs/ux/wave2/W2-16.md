# W2-16 · Copy focus: short explanations, in place
**One line:** working screens lead with what to do, the ₪ and the status; every explanation stays on the screen where it is needed but is cut, shortened to one sentence, shared as one string, or folded behind a small in-place "למה?" / "פרטים" / "איך חישבנו?" toggle. One `<Explain>` fold, one `explain` namespace for ideas that repeat across screens, and a cheap guard that keeps it that way. Today the in-app screens carry 7,303 words of rendered explanation (892 inventoried strings); Batch P (train B, values only) takes it to 6,393; this package and its adopters take it to ≈4,420 (−39%), and remove three explanations that contradict each other (what counts in the leak, whether pending invoices count, a false "the owner was notified" on every page). Protects the owner's trust in the one ₪ number and the receiver's minutes at the dock. **Owner agent:** web-feature-engineer (catalogs and screens); rtl-design-steward (the fold toggle's visuals and tokens); test-sentinel (the guard test and the e2e budget). **Device:** both. **Effort:** M. **humanPR:** no.

- **The pattern (MECHANISM.md, in short):**
  1. Each explanation gets one treatment, decided in this order: keep (legal, ₪/destructive confirmation, blocking reason, deadline, approvals `WhyLine`, a11y name) → cut (the screen already says it) → share (the idea appears on another screen: one key) → shorten (≤12 words and needed every time) → fold (longer, or needed only sometimes). On receiver and chef field screens prefer cut over fold.
  2. **The fold is one component, `<Explain>`**: a visible text button ("למה?" / "פרטים" / "איך חישבנו?") at the inline end of the line it explains; ≥44 px (48 px inside `[data-mode=field]`); `aria-expanded` + `aria-controls`; the panel opens inline below the row, starts closed on server and client (no CLS), focus stays on the toggle, never a popover or a sheet; the body is plain text with no ₪, links or buttons; at most one fold per card/row and three per screen; never inside a `ConfirmDialog`, `Toast`, `Banner`, `EmptyState`, an error, `WhyLine`, `DeadlineChip` or a legal block. No remember-open: a fold people keep opening means a short version belongs on the line.
  3. **One string per repeated idea:** used by 2+ namespaces → `explain.<concept>` in the new namespace (no ₪, ≤40 words, ≤12 per sentence, so receiver and chef can see every one); used by one namespace → `<ns>.explain.<concept>` in that catalog.
  4. **A guard:** a unit test caps catalog strings by class (visible 12 words, folded 40, toast 8, button 4) with a ratchet allowlist and fails on exact duplicates; an e2e word budget per screen type ratchets down.
  **No help centre, no glossary page, no tooltips.** W2-13's `GLOSSARY` stays a code-side vocabulary for its copy-gate; any in-app "מה זה?" renders through `<Explain>` inline, and a glossary `define` that matches an `explain.<concept>` points to it, so each text is written once (confirm with W2-13 on day 1).
- **Owner decisions that bind** (README §7, §12): the terms חשבונית מרכזת (never "ריכוז"), פער (never "חריגה"/"חריגות"), דרישת זיכוי (never "תביעה"), קבלת סחורה (never bare "קבלה" for goods), "דליפה" only for the monthly leak, price creep = "מחירים שעלו"; no "יעד" and no 1.5% target anywhere; receiver sees no purchase or leak ₪ anywhere; the chef sees ₪ only on the orders screens and in recipes (31.10, README §12); T4 pending/מרכזת lines stay calm and neutral; destructive and money confirmations name the ₪ and the supplier and are never folded or cut; a11y names may be shortened, never removed; legal text untouched; plural imperative ("שלחו", "בדקו"), noun forms for the fixed actions ("שמירה", "ביטול"), no slash forms; MISSING_ON_INVOICE is out of the leak from train B (so `explain.leakRule` lists "שורת הזמנה לא חויבה" as not counted); an overdue claim reminder is a draft that sends only on a click (`explain.noSendWithoutTap`); light "command center for money" design, tokens only.
