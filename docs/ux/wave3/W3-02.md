## 4. Slices
**a · Workbench (this PR)**
1. **IA-02 bug (first):** `recipes/page.tsx` renders `PeriodCard` only for owner/manager (the roles `inventory.period` serves). The chef sees dishes first, never an error card.
2. **`recipes.preview`** (query, chef+, flag-gated, **no write**): takes a draft recipe (the `save` shape, `id` optional) and returns plate cost, batch cost, net menu price, food-cost %, margin, the gap to target in bp, the traffic light, and per-line cost + status + share of plate (bp, largest-remainder so the shares sum to exactly 10,000 when the plate cost is > 0). A foreign or unknown `id`, product id or sub-recipe id → `NOT_FOUND` (same tokens as `save`); a non-sub-recipe used as one → `BAD_REQUEST notSubRecipe`. Unit mismatches and missing prices come back as line statuses, not errors (the chef is mid-typing). Cycles are costed as `cycle` lines. Pure logic in `packages/api/src/recipes/preview.ts`.
3. **Live editor:** a 250 ms debounced `recipes.preview` drives the aside (and a sticky bottom summary on phone): plate cost, food %, margin, gap to target, and a share-of-plate bar per line. Lines show their live cost. Invalid quantities are skipped and marked on the line, never sent.
4. **"נסה בלי לשמור" sandbox:** a toggle that marks the editor as a trial (no save button, a "חזרה למתכון השמור" reset). The preview never writes.
5. **Decimal input:** quantities, waste and menu price go through the `parseDecimalInput` interim ("1,5" = 1.5 for a quantity; an ambiguous "1,250" is flagged, never guessed).
6. **IA-02 on the recipes home:** tiles 4 → 2 ("מנות מעל הרצוי"; "חסר מחיר" only when > 0); the target shown once.

**a2 (same branch, follow-up commits, as dependencies land):** the ARIA ingredient combobox (keyboard pick focuses qty) · dense desktop grid with H1 = dish name · role-branched home (chef: dishes needing attention first) · `PriceMove` old ← new + ₪/month + "הצע מחיר תפריט" · audited recipe history · phone read-only service card with portion scaling · one dish alert line (IA-02 §3).

**b · Inventory + control room (after W2-07 on main):** period sparkline, the clickable equation (purchases → invoices, credits → credit requests, stock → counts), category split, top-10 unexplained usage; IA-02 §4-5 (price source behind "למה?", "תיקון" into ⋯).

## 5. Acceptance (slice a)
- The chef opens `/dashboard/recipes` with `inventoryCount` on: no error card, no `inventory.period` call.
- Typing a quantity moves plate cost, food %, margin and the gap within 250 ms of the last keystroke + the round trip, with no row written (`recipes` / `recipe_items` unchanged; asserted in the test).
- Shares of plate sum to exactly 100.00% when the plate cost is > 0; missing-price lines show as such and the summary says "חלקי".
- ₪ only through `ils()` → `formatIls`; % through `pct()`. No new `Intl.NumberFormat`.
- Hebrew-first RTL, AA pairs, tiers: the plate cost is the hero of the card; an over-target dish is T2 warn; on target T3 calm; no price T4 neutral.
- The chef sees ₪ here (recipes are exempt, owner decision 6). The receiver never reaches the route.
