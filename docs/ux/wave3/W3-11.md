# W3-11 · Document intelligence: credit notes and supplier statements

**One line:** a supplier's credit note closes the right credit requests in integer agorot ("אשר שיוך"), and a supplier statement (כרטסת) is reconciled line by line against what we hold, with every gap turned into a flag. A credit note is never a short-pay.

## 4. Slices
- **a (now, non-locked):**
  1. `recovery/suggest.ts` — pure `suggestAllocation(noteAgorot, claims)`: exact single claim → exact one invoice → exact subset (bounded) → oldest first. Integer agorot; Σ allocations ≤ note; never touches a claim of another supplier. `recovery.suggestAllocation` query. Confirm = existing `recovery.recordCreditNote` ("אשר שיוך").
  2. `ledger/statement.ts` — pure `reconcileStatement(statement, ours)` + `ledger/statementCsv.ts` parser. Flags: `invoice_never_got`, `billed_not_received`, `amount_mismatch`, `credit_missing`, `credit_unrecorded` (T3 good news: record it), `payment_unapplied`. Credit lines match credit notes only, never netted against an invoice or payment (no short-pay). `suppliers.reconcileStatement` (stateless, takes the raw CSV, parsed server-side; no storage).

## 5. Acceptance (slice a)
- A suggestion never allocates more than the note or more than a claim's open amount, prefers an exact match, and is deterministic. "אשר שיוך" moves exactly the suggested claims to `credited` through `recordCreditNote`.
- Statement reconciliation finds each of the 6 flags on a fixture, ₪ exact to the agora, and a credit line is never reported as a payment or short-pay.
- New procedures are finance-only (receiver/chef see no ₪), flag-gated like their router, tenant-scoped; a foreign supplier id → NOT_FOUND.
- Hebrew-first RTL, AA, ₪ only via `formatIls()`; supplier wording per GLOSSARY (בקשת זיכוי, חשבונית זיכוי; never דרישה / תביעה / מחלוקת / חוב / באיחור).
