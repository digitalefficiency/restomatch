# WA-08 · Owner, manager and bookkeeper on WhatsApp: the ₪ waiting for you, and the button to act
**One line:** the owner gets a 07:30 brief (gaps to decide and their ₪, claims overdue after 7 days, the month's leak), decides gaps one card at a time with WA-07's amount-named confirm, asks "כמה דלף אצל…" and gets a grounded answer, and sends claim letters **from their own WhatsApp**. The manager gets the same decision cards for what they may act on, late-truck alerts with a "ask the supplier from your phone" link, and unknown-document triage. The bookkeeper gets a Sunday digest, close status and export **links** (never files). **Read-only first (E3), decisions a week later (E4).**

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **O1 is read-only, skipped on empty days, never on Shabbat**, ≤1 per owner per day, perishable after 12:00; ₪ via `moneyText`; month leak excludes MISSING_ON_INVOICE (a seed with only such lines gives a month leak of 0 and no "דליפה" ₪ figure counts them).
2. **Cards show the exact gap** and never offer "לתבוע" for MISSING_ON_INVOICE / PRICE_LOWER / UNORDERED_ARRIVAL.
3. **Decisions only through WA-07** (test: no flow calls `approvals.approve` directly).
4. **Manager sees only what they may act on** (W2-04 `canAct`); owner-required items are information only.
5. **Nothing reaches a supplier from our number:** O4, M2 and S1 are CTAs to the person's own WhatsApp; test: 0 outbox rows whose target is a supplier contact.
6. **7-day overdue** counts from `letter_sent`; `rm_claim_overdue` fires once at day 7 via AU-03's 08:30 run (no duplicate from WA-08).
7. **Exports are links only**, to the signed-in web; a "send me the file" request gets the link reply.
8. **Rollout gate:** PR b's decision cards are behind an option `whatsappDecisions` (default off) until one clean E3 week; with it off the brief button opens the queue read-only with a web link.
9. **Grounded answers:** every ₪ in O3/K2 equals a tool figure (WA-02 grounding); else the facts renderer.
10. **Tenancy and roles:** bookkeeper sees bookkeeper-routed items only; a manager of A asking about B's supplier → "לא מצאתי ספק בשם הזה".
