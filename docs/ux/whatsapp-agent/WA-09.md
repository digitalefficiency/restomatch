# WA-09 · The chef on WhatsApp: "order like last week" becomes a draft, and the chef sends it from their own phone
**One line:** the chef writes "תזמין כמו בשבוע שעבר מירקות השרון", sees a preview of last week's lines with quantities and an estimated total (₪ is allowed on orders), edits by text ("עגבניות 50, בלי פטרוזיליה"), taps "צור טיוטה", and then sends the order **from their own WhatsApp** through a prefilled `wa.me` link. A cut-off reminder says "טיוטה איננה הזמנה" when a draft is still unsent. The chef never sees leak, decision or claim ₪; RestoMatch never messages the supplier.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **The agent never calls `orders.placeOrder`** (test: spy on the caller; 0 calls in all chef flows) and **0 outbox rows target a supplier**.
2. **Draft only:** after "צור טיוטה" the PO is `draft`; the text says "עוד לא נשלחה"; nothing else changes.
3. **Own send:** the CTA URL is `https://wa.me/<supplier digits>?text=<encoded order text>`; with no supplier number it is the hint, not a share picker.
4. **₪ allowed on orders only:** the preview shows line and estimated totals; a chef asking for leak, decisions, claims, VAT or the invoice total gets the C3 refusal and no tool call returns those fields (redaction test on `owner.*`, `approvals.*`, `recovery.*`, `receiving.getInvoice`).
5. **`rm_order_cutoff` has no ₪ variable** (registry test, WA-06ʰ) and the chef gets ≤1 cut-off reminder per supplier per day.
6. **Edits parse like the web:** the same text through `captureFromText` and through the chat yields the same lines (shared-parser test).
7. **Ambiguity:** two supplier matches → list "למי התכוונת?"; none → "לא מצאתי ספק בשם הזה" + list of up to 10 suppliers.
8. **Cut-off honesty:** a draft created yesterday and still unsent is "not ordered"; a draft sent today is ordered (AU-04's `sent_at` rule, read-only).
9. **Tenancy:** a chef of A cannot see B's POs (two-tenant test).
10. **Off switch:** `whatsappAgent` off or the per-restaurant option `whatsappChef` off → chef bindings get the off reply.

## 8. Notes + Traps
- **Trap:** `placeOrder({channel:'whatsapp'})` looks like the obvious "send" — it sends from **our** number to the supplier. Forbidden in v1 (owner rule: nothing to suppliers automatically). Only the chef's own tap in their own WhatsApp sends.
- **Trap:** "last week" means the last **sent** PO for that supplier on the same weekday, not the last draft; use W2-06's guide when there is none.
- **Trap:** a prepared draft from AU-11 may already exist for tomorrow; offer it ("יש כבר טיוטה מוכנה") instead of creating a second one.
- **Trap:** quantities typed in Hebrew units (ק״ג, צרור, ארגז) must map to the PO line's unit; on a mismatch ask, do not guess.
- **Trap:** the chef may also be a manager (WA-04 least privilege): the chef role is active until they switch.
- Recipe cost questions join only when W3-02 ships a procedure that G-07 marks chef-visible (design §B.3 C3).
