## 4. What to build
- **Kill-switch levels:** (1) global `FEATURES_OFF=whatsappAgent` stops every agent send and model call within one request; (2) per restaurant `whatsappAgent` flag, owner-toggleable, plus options for decisions, chef and receiver; (3) per person = WA-04's "עצור" / "נתק" (read here, not rebuilt); (4) **model-off mode**: buttons, templates and the deterministic router keep working, free text gets the canned intents (`ask/offline.ts`) rendered by WA-02, entered automatically on budget exhaustion, provider outage, or the daily cap.
- **Cost meter** (`costMeter.ts`): per restaurant per Israel day: templates and service messages by `cost_category` (from `wa_messages`), model micro-USD (from WA-02 turns), converted at a configured FX (ASSUMPTION ₪3.7/$). Exposed to AU-07 and to the admin only (not a staff score).
- **Daily cap** (`dailyCap.ts`): default `waDailyCapUsd` = 1.00 per restaurant (≈ 3× the realistic daily spend, ASSUMPTION); at the cap → model-off for that restaurant until 06:00, one in-app notice to the owner.
- **Alerts to a human** (`alerts.ts`, to the ops channel, never to staff): template re-categorised; quality rating drop; delivery success <95% for an hour; no inbound for 2 hours on a weekday morning while sends succeed (silent webhook); Meta billing failure or balance below a floor.
- **Metrics** (`metrics.ts`, events for AU-07): per turn (hashed binding, role, intent, tools, latency, tokens, cached tokens, cost, grounded, refusal, staged/committed/expired, outbound ids; **no message text**); per day per restaurant (sends by category, failure rate by error code, opt-outs, budget exhaustion, model-off minutes). ## 5. Acceptance criteria
1. **Level 1:** with `FEATURES_OFF=whatsappAgent`, 0 model calls and 0 agent sends across all flows; statuses still recorded; T2 items still reach web/push/e-mail.
2. **Level 2:** a restaurant with the flag off gets the off reply; another restaurant is unaffected (two-tenant test).
3. **Level 4:** at the daily cap or on a provider error the next free text gets a canned answer and a button still commits through WA-07.
4. **Cost meter** totals equal the sum of `wa_messages` by category and WA-02 turn costs for a seeded day (to the micro-USD).
5. **No staff monitoring:** per-person response times are never shown on any page; metrics carry hashed binding ids only.
6. **Alerts fire once per incident** and only to the ops channel (test each of the five triggers).
7. **Receiver option guard:** `whatsappReceiver` cannot be switched on unless WA-10's attack test is GREEN on `main` and the H-11 production date is ≥14 days ago (config constant set by a human).
8. **COSTS.md:** the WhatsApp line sits under "Operating cost vs. ARPU", is labelled a cost, and is not added to any leak or hours figure.
9. **AU-01 row:** RTL, WCAG AA, keyboard, he-IL; owner-only toggle; a manager sees the state read-only.
